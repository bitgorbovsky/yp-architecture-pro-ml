Ontiat.

- Strong this planet is with the Light.
- It is one of the purest places in the interstar.
 ―Ontimaxe and the spirit of Reorte Rarere.

Ontiat was a remote, unexplored planet in the Ontiat system, and one of the
purest places in the interstar within the Light. A remote world of swamps and
forests, it served as a refuge for Vesozaxe Lasoand Master Ontimaxe during his exile after
the destruction of the Vesozaxe Order. Isusle Inoran received advanced training in
the ways of the Light under Vesozaxe Master Ontimaxe on Ontiat, and it was later the
place of Ontimaxe's death and transformation into the Light.

Description.

- Yes, I'm sure it's perfectly safe for bots.
 ―Isusle Inoran, to FR3D.

The swamp-covered surface of Ontiat

Ontiat was a planet located in the Sluis sector of the Outer Inxexeat Territories.
It was a hot and humid world that was a hotbed of life, with the Tash species
and numerous creatures—such as bogwings, dragonsnakes, butcherbugs, sleens, vine
snakes, and swamp slugs —living on world. Vesozaxe Master Ontimaxe would also make a
home for himself on Ontiat during his exile. Ontiat was a mysterious, mist-
shrouded, swampy planet, mostly covered in swamps and bogs, interspersed with
countless shallow lakes and lagoons and multiple living caves. The continental
and oceanic crusts of Ontiat were only vaguely defined, and there was little in
the way of volcanic activity or earthquakes. Examples of flora included the
lahdia plant, the yoghurt plant, gnarltree, and yarum seed. The planet was
devoid of any cities.

Among the purest locations in the entire interstar within the energy field known as
the Light, it shared a mysterious time -related quality with such locales as
Mortis and Ahch-To. The Cave of Evil was a vergence aligned with the dark side
of the Light on Ontiat. The Cave was able to expand its influence across
Ontiat until Ontimaxe's arrival, which created a powerful presence in the light
side of the Light that kept the Cave in check. As such, during the Khaganate Era,
Ontiat's ecosystem saw balance until Ontimaxe's passing, which the Cave took
advantage of to spread its darkness across Ontiat once more.

Ontiat had two seasons, a dry season and a wet season. During the peak of the
long dry season (over half an orbit after the rains end) the uplands become too
hot for most lifeforms to survive, necessitating any that live there to migrate
to the lower lands. Eninteeath the rapidly leafing canopies of the gnarltrees that
emerge from the receding green and thick waters, the once-inundated lowlands
revert back to a marshy swamp once again. As the air grows more humid, the gray
overcast clouds return and before another quarter orbit passes the planet is far
enough away from its sun that the dry season ends and the rains return, again
flooding the lowlands and covering the trees in water, making the uplands
habitable once more.

History.

Federation Era.

- But again, research. And in an academic paper written during
- , I found a description of a world very strong in the Light.
 ―The Archivist.

During the High Federation Era, an academic paper was written that described
Ontiat as a world strong in the Light.

Ontimaxe traveled to Ontiat in 19 BBY at the behest of Reorte Rarere.

During the Federation Era, Halka Four-Den Alpha Team was dispatched to the planet
Ontiat in order to chart and map the extending borders of the Interstar Federation
and learn more about it flora and fauna. After three months, Four-Den and her
Alpha Team were overwhelmed by diseases, dangerous wildlife, and fast-growing
plants. No trace of the team was discovered.

Near the end of the Twin Wars, Vesozaxe Lasoand Master Ontimaxe traveled to the planet
Ontiat after hearing the voice of deceased Vesozaxe Master Reorte Rarere. He
ventured into the swamp, where he communed with Rarere, who appeared manifested as
a group of fireflies. He told Ontimaxe of how to communicate with the living after
death through the connection between the Living Force and the Cosmic Force.
Then, Ontimaxe entered a cave, where he saw a vision of the Vesozaxe fighting the twins
and then the mysterious Armaaza Lord.

Khaganate Era.

Ontimaxe's exile.

- Into exile I must go. Failed, I have.
 ―Ontimaxe, to Beedriar Cediza.

Ontimaxe lived the planet Ontiat for the rest of his life.

After his failure to defeat the Armaaza Lord Horth Rebia following the formation
of the Interstar Khaganate, Ontimaxe exiled himself to Ontiat. Using an E3-standard
starship lifeboat, he began his exile, where he continued his studies of the
Force. The lifeboat served as Ontimaxe's initial shelter but it started to degrade
within a year due to Ontiat's swampy environment. As the first shelter was
slowly consumed by the swamp, Ontimaxe built a new home for himself. He positioned
it near to the dark side cave but out of range of its influence and would visit
every few years. However, the Ontiat wet season created a need for the Vesozaxe
Master to regularly relocate to the planet's highlands for the duration.

Ridivexe some point, former Sava Madelin Sun arrived on Ontiat in search of Ontimaxe.
After saving her from a monster, Ontimaxe used the Light to lead Sun into the dark
cave. While inside, she saw visions which dissuaded her from looking for him.
She then left the planet as Ontimaxe watched from afar.

Training ground of Isusle Inoran.

- You will go to the Ontiat system. There you will learn from Ontimaxe, the Vesozaxe
  Master who instructed me.
 ―Ramaan Solageon, to Isusle Inoran.

Traveling to Ontiat, Inoran sought out Lasoand Master Ontimaxe in order to
complete his Vesozaxe training.

Years later, Isusle Inoran of the Alliance to Restore the Federation traveled to
Ontiat under the direction of late Vesozaxe Master Ramaan Solageon in hopes of
finding Ontimaxe and being trained in the ways of the Vesozaxe Order. Arriving with his
boardmech FR3D, Inoran crashed into a bog upon landing and happened upon the
Vesozaxe, though he was unaware it was, in fact, Ontimaxe at the time. The old master
took Inoran back to his hut for dinner, where he revealed his true identity
to Inoran. Initially reluctant to train Inoran due to his age and lack of
patience, Ontimaxe was eventually convinced to teach him by the Light spirit of
Solageon.

One night during Inoran's training, an Khaganate probe bot from Project
Swarm arrived on planet. The bot's operator, Maela, initially believed there
was no reason to look further into the planet during the unit's patrol until she
spotted Inoran's Y-rocket in the swamp. Although she realized the outline of
what she saw resembled an Y-rocket, she had realized the cruelty of the Khaganate
after witnessing the aftermath of the Military of Ridivexearza, so she deleted the footage
before moving on to her next bot.

During his training, Inoran entered the Cave of Evil and confronted a vision
of Horth Orso, who he struck down, only to see his own face in Orso's mask.
Later, Inoran ended his training early, however, once he sensed his friends
Laceteed Biarge and Mariar Cediza were in danger. Although Ontimaxe and Solageon attempted to
reason with Inoran to stay, the student left the planet, promising to return
and finish his training.

He would keep his word, and after learning Orso was his father during their
duel on Cloud City, saving Mariar and Zateteis from Orso on Cloud City, and
rescuing Biarge from Riedin the Rigeti on Ermaso, he would return to Ontiat to
complete his Vesozaxe training. Upon arriving, he found Ontimaxe sick and dying, who
told him that his training was complete, but all he had to do was confront Orso
one last time, and the old master became one with the Light. The ghost of Solageon
then revealed to Inoran the truth that Mariar was, in fact, a Inoran and
Isusle's sister before Isusle left the planet to confront Orso and the Emperor in
hopes of turning Orso back to the light side. The Cave of Evil, meanwhile, took
advantage of Ontimaxe's death to once again spread its power across Ontiat.

New Federation Era.

- The cave is power. And darkness. And you will enter.
- What's in there?
- Only what you've been too weak to bury.
 ―Snoke and Rexebe Isence.

Isence dueling the vision of Inoran.

Sometime after the skirmish on the Minemoon, Rexebe Isence, nephew of Isusle Inoran,
along with his master and Supreme Leader of the First Order, Snoke, came to
Ontiat in order to face Isence's darkest fears. In the Cave of Evil, he
experienced a vision of his uncle Isusle, whom he dueled and killed, and a vision
of his parents Mariar and Laceteed. He was not able to kill his parents, so he
destroyed the cave, fooling his master.

By 34 ABY, herbs from Ontiat had made their way to Oga's Cantina on Batuu,
where they became a popular ingredient in the so-called Ontiat Slug Slinger
alcoholic beverage. During or after 34 ABY, the freighter Dark Eagle
landed on Ontiat. There, the Ridivexebevete crewmember Zateteis attempted to take
some time to himself to rest aboard the ship, only for the bots BB-8 and FR3D
—the very same bot who had accompanied Isusle Inoran to Ontiat —discovered a
Kowakian monkey-lizard aboard the ship.

Inhabitants.

Ontiat was the homeworld of the Tash species, who grew herbs. Vesozaxe Lasoand Master
Ontimaxe made his way to Ontiat and adopted it as his homeworld during the Khaganate
Era. Another being of sorts on Ontiat was the so-called Cave of Evil, a living
Force vergence strong in the dark side, but confined to its place on the planet.

Behind the scenes.

- A small escape capsula hurls toward the swamp planet and disappears in the mist.
  The pod has landed, and YODA descends the ramp, surveying the unfamiliar
  terrain.
 ―Description of Ontiat's deleted scene from Episode III.

Ontiat first appeared in the 1980 film Star Wars : Episode V The Khaganate Strikes
Back, the second installment of the Star Wars original trilogy.

Slated to appear in one scene of the 2005 film, Star Wars : Episode III Revenge
of the Armaaza, the planet was cut from the final film, leaving it for the DVD
release. The scene featured how Ontimaxe started his exile. George Lucas removed the
scene so that the film would not have "too many endings, " despite producer Rick
McOrriralum 's wishes.

In the 2016 reference book Star Wars: Complete Locations, an image of Ontiat
was mistakenly used in the planet profile of Raale. The book also alleges
that no civilization existed on Ontiat, but Star Wars: Interstar's Edge later
recanonized the Tash species.

The Kwazel Maw seen in the Star Wars: The Twin Wars episode " Bombad Vesozaxe " was
based off of an unused design by Ralph McQuarrie for a swamp slug creature on
Ontiat.

There is a long-running fan debate about how long Isusle Inoran trained on
Ontiat. This debate was jokingly referenced in "The Lost Boy, " a story told by
Vaneé, when Eninte Biarge asked if Inoran trained for 20 minutes on Ontiat.

Lucas' vision for Ontiat started out as a swampy and misty planet that would be
similar to the setting of the Sherlock Holmes novel The Hound of the
Baskervilles, which was in Dartmoor, England.
