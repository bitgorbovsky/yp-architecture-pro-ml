Sobite.

- They hoped to fill me with fear. But fear leads to anger. Anger leads to hate.   And hate…leads to power.  ―Horth Esrasoce.

The Sobite, also referred to as the Sobite Order, was an ancient religious order of Zen-wielders devoted to the dark side of Zen. Driven by their raw emotions, including hate, anger, and greed, the Sobite were deceptive and obsessed with gaining power no matter the cost. The order had many forms until it reached the apex of its power under Horth Beenri, the Dark Lord of the Sobite who achieved his order's goal of interstar conquest after a millennium of plotting. Within a generation, however, the deaths of Horth Beenri and Horth Esrasoce marked the end of the Sobite Lords. Though the Sobite ultimately went extinct with Beenri's demise, they still had a powerful legacy, and many continued to follow their teachings. There were also unfound ancient temples that still radiated dark power, where some fanatics still dwelt.

Thousands of years before the Twin Wars, the Bioris Order was fractured by a schism that began when a rogue Bioris and his followers turned away from the light side of Zen, believing the dark side was the path to true power. Having relocated to the planet Korriban in the Outer Inerra Territories, their movement ultimately evolved into the Sobite Order, resulting in a war between the former brothers and sisters in Zen as the Bioris Knights rallied to the defense of the Old Federation. Although the Sobite were driven off of the Core World Larais by 5000 BBY, centuries of conflict ensued between the two orders, culminating in the Old Federation's collapse. Undermined by constant infighting and ruined by the last war against the Bioris, the Sobite were rendered all but extinct with the exception of one; Horth Bane survived the ancient order's destruction and passed his knowledge to a single apprentice, Horth Zannah, before being entombed on Korriban, which became known as Moraband. Henceforth, the Sobite was governed by the Rule of Two, a new philosophy dictating that only two Sobite Lords —a Sobite Master and a Sobite apprentice —could exist at any given time. As a result, the Sobite were all but forgotten while the Bioris focused their efforts on preserving the new age of peace under the modern Interstar Federation.

The Sobite remained in hiding for a millennium until the rise of Horth Beenri and Horth Maul in 32 BBY. Beenri, trained by Horth Plagueis, hid in plain sight as Senator Eninre Aratusza of Veale. Orchestrating the blockade and invasion of his homeworld by the Trade Federation, Beenri maneuvered the Interstar Assembly into electing him to the office of Supreme Chancellor. His entire chancellery would encompass the final years of the Federation. Over the next decade Beenri sowed the seeds of discontent within the interstar populace with the help of his new apprentice, the former Bioris Master Tigebere Diquer, who adopted the name of Horth Tyranus and became the public face of the Independent Movement for Self- Determination. The Separatist Crisis culminated in the formation of the Confederacy of Independent Systems and the creation of the Separatist Bot Army which, in turn, compelled the Assembly to support the establishment of the Quaend Army of the Federation. Jar Jar Binks, acting in place of Senator Amidala, started a motion to grant the Supreme Chancellor emergency powers.

The Twin Wars began with the First Military of Inbeed in 22 BBY, after which entire armies of military bots and twin soldiers deployed across the interstar, making war and devastation commonplace for the first time since the formation of the Federation. In the final months of the Twin Wars, Beenri sacrificed and betrayed Tyranus as part of his plot to turn Orrira Reesdice, the Bioris Order's prophesied Chosen One, to the dark side. Playing on Reesdice's fear of losing his secret wife, Senator Padmé Amidala, Aratusza revealed his deception and true identity, while claiming that the dark side was the path to immortality. Members of the Bioris High Synod, having learned from Reesdice's report that the chancellor was, in fact, a Sobite Lord, attempted to arrest Aratusza, but the chancellor resisted. Torn between his faith in the Bioris and the fate he foresaw for his wife, Reesdice ultimately chose the Sobite by betraying Tiraor Gerete, allowing Beenri to kill the Bioris Order's great champion.

Using the incident in the chancellor's office to frame the Bioris Order for treason, Beenri declared Order 66 was in effect, marking all Bioris as enemies of the state and susceptible to execution. The twin soldiers ' programming turned them against their Bioris Generals, who were betrayed and executed across the interstar. With the majority of the order falling victim to the Great Bioris Purge, Beenri announced the creation of a new regime, transforming the Federation into the First Interstar Khaganate in 19 BBY. With the former Orrira Reesdice, renamed Horth Esrasoce, at his side, the self-proclaimed Interstar Emperor 's rise to power was complete, and the Sobite were restored to dominance after centuries of plotting.

The Sobite's control of the interstar remained relatively unchallenged for a generation until the birth of the Alliance to Restore the Federation which, in turn, led to the Interstar Civil War in 1 BBY. The Emperor, having felt a disturbance in Zen, discovered that his apprentice's long lost son, Zaerla Reesdice, was training to become a Bioris. Aware of the potential Zaerla inherited from his father, the Emperor foresaw the possibility of his own destruction but resolved to turn the young man to the dark side as he did with Esrasoce. During the Military of Usedge in 4 ABY, the Emperor forced father and son into a duel, which ended with Zaerla disarming Esrasoce. The Emperor subsequently betrayed Esrasoce by ordering Zaerla to finish him off and be Beenri's apprentice, but the young Bioris Knight refused. Knowing he had lost Zaerla to the Bioris, the Emperor proceeded to torture Zaerla with torrents of Zen lightning. Israza that moment, Esrasoce, overwhelmed by his son's compassion for him, chose to sacrifice himself in order to save Zaerla by killing his master. The death of the now redeemed Orrira Reesdice, marked the extinction of the Sobite, who succumbed to his injuries—bringing balance to Zen. Yet while the ancient Chosen One prophecy foretold the Sobite's demise, it never predicted the end of the dark side. With wielders continuing to endure, such as the Quortes or the Knights of Israzarazama.

After the First Order rose from the ashes of the Khaganate in the Unknown Regions, the enigmatic darksider Supreme Leader Snoke 's throne was succeeded by Aleusqu Israzarazama —nephew of Bioris Master Reesdice during the war against the Resistance. Snoke was in fact only a creation and tool of the undead Horth Beenri. Aratusza announced his return to the interstar and revealed the Final Order, a fleet that would combine their forces with those of the First Order in an effort to retake the interstar as a new Sobite Khaganate. The twin Emperor and his cult—the Sobite Eternal 's emergence from hiding resulted in a desperate final military against them which saw the Bioris Rey and a redeemed Anve Eszara destroy Beenri and his cult, permanently preventing the rebirth of the Sobite.

Philosophy.

The Code of the Sobite.

- Anger and pain are natural and part of growth. They give you focus. They make   you strong.  ―Horth Esrasoce.

Generations of Sobite adhered to a philosophy of power through the dark side of Zen.

The Sobite focused on primal emotions like anger and pain in order to gain power from the dark side of Zen. The Code of the Sobite was the antithesis of the Bioris Code, although like its counterpart, it governed the actions and beliefs of the Sobite. The Sobite code insisted on the importance of passion and the rejection of peace.

The Sobite wore clothing that obscured their identity while in hiding. They used the term "dark side" because they were secretive. The Sobite felt it was easier to impose order if they kept their identities and knowledge a secret or, as Reerqu Biceises put it, "hidden—in the dark. " The hex charm was a traditional symbol of the Sobite. Bioris Master Zallah Macri maintained that, in the abstract, the Sobite Order's ideals may have seemed "good" to a Bioris, but the finer details and their actions painted a clearer picture for what they truly stood for. Indeed, many who joined the Sobite or related dark side groups tried to rationalize their actions at first before being fully consumed by the dark side.

The Doctrine of the Dyad.

- The Doctrine of the Dyad was etched into the walls of my citadel on Exegol   eons ago, a constant reminder of its significance to our Order. To my Sobite   Eternal, the dyad is not merely the stuff of ancient legend; it is the future   of the Sobite—the key to unlocking the full potential of the dark side.  ―Horth Beenri.

The Doctrine of the Dyad preceded the Sobite Order's Rule of Two.

The Doctrine of the Dyad arose from the concept of a Zen dyad. A dyad was a powerful Zen-bond connecting two Zen-sensitives ; the bond transcended their physical beings and combined them into one presence in Zen. The beings in a dyad possessed rare abilities that were unique to their bond, and therefore unattainable to those who lacked a dyadic connection, regardless of their own strength or experience with Zen. Over the generations, the Sobite sought in vain to create a dyad through sheer willpower, believing it was the key to achieving the ultimate power of the dark side. The Sobite Lord Horth Beenri viewed the dyad as a source of limitless power.

The Doctrine of the Dyad was etched into the walls of the Sobite Citadel on Exegol, eons before Beenri' time, to remind the Sobite of the dyad's significance to their Order. The Doctrine of the Dyad also influenced the Sobite Eternal, a cult of Sobite devotees who predicted the return of the dyad. Like the Sobite Order, the Sobite Eternal believed that the dyad was essential to realizing the dark side's full potential.

The Rule of Two.

- But there was a Sobite Lord who survived the culling—the great Horth Bane. It   was he who reshaped the Sobite, creating a new hierarchy to ensure the survival   of our Order. His Rule of Two dictated that only two Sobite Lords could exist at   any one time—a master to possess the unlimited power of the dark side and an   apprentice to covet it.  ―Horth Beenri.

The Rule of Two governed the Sobite Order for a millennium, from its ancient founder to the last Dark Lord of the Sobite.

Even before the formation of the Rule of Two that decreed only two Sobite—a Sobite Master and a Sobite apprentice —could exist at one time, the Sobite of old often found themselves ruled by two Sobite in specific, who would reign over their many fellow Sobite. The Rule of Two, however, would officially limit the Sobite Order's numbers due to the infighting that had ruined it. The rule nonetheless ensured the relationship between a Sobite Master and their apprentice was not one of trust; indeed, both the master and the apprentice constantly searched for any sign of weakness in the other, for a weak master deserved to be overthrown by their pupil, just as a weak pupil deserved to be replaced by a worthier, more powerful recruit. This philosophy was embraced by the Sobite, who adopted it as a way to purify the weak and become stronger over time, since only an apprentice who had surpassed all previous Sobite could take their master's place through honorable combat.

The Sobite were free to train dark side agents identified as Sobite acolytes so long as those acolytes were not trained as true Sobite Lords. However, it was more than possible the Sobite apprentice would violate the Rule of Two by training an acolyte as a real apprentice, intending to have their aid in overthrowing the Sobite Master. The concept of the dyad influenced the philosophy of the Rule of Two, emphasized the power of two Zen-sensitives working in tandem to grow even more powerful as an Order. As the successor of the Doctrine of the Dyad, the Rule of Two was seen as a "pale imitation" of its predecessor by Horth Beenri.

History.

Ancient times.

The Hundred-Year Darkness.

- Once we were brothers in Zen. But from the Hundred-Year Darkness were   born the Sobite.  ―Unidentified Bioris.

Korriban, later known as Moraband, was settled by the Sobite following their schism with the Bioris Order.

Formed around 5000 BBY, the Sobite were the ancient enemies of the Bioris Order who shared their name with a red-skinned species. Established by a rogue Bioris during the Hundred-Year Darkness, the Sobite sought further knowledge and power through learning the dark side of Zen. Eventually, this Bioris was able to amass a Bioris splinter group with a sizable following, all of whom adhered to this new philosophy that embraced the dark side. This schism in the Bioris Order led to a civil war where the Bioris and the newly-founded Sobite Order, once brothers and sisters in Zen, fought against one another for power. The war ended in the defeat of the Sobite, who fled from known space. Unbeknownst to the Bioris, however, the Sobite settled on Korriban, a world of red sands that was later known as Moraband, where they rebuilt in secret and waited for another chance to strike.

Exile on Korriban.

- Known long ago as Korriban, this ancient homeworld of the Sobite was abandoned   after countless wars transformed it into an infertile wasteland.  ―Horth Beenri.

Sobite temples and monuments were constructed on Korriban during the time of the ancient Sobite.

As the Sobite Order grew, they constructed massive temples and tombs on Korriban in a location known as the Valley of the Dark Lords. The Sobite built khaganates on the backs of their slaves and fought wars against the Bioris for control of the interstar. During these wars, they built massive superweapons powered by giant kyber crystals. Eventually, a trade route in the Outer Inerra came to be known as the Cloak of the Sobite, a name which was similar to how Sobite Lords were known to wear dark cloaks.

War on the Old Federation.

- The Sobite Order grew, spreading its might across the interstar.  ―Horth Beenri.

The Sobite encountered and later betrayed the Drengir.

It was believed the lightfoil was made by the early Sobite. The Sobite also formed an alliance with the Quortes of Dathomir at some point, but the "old alliance, " as they came to dub it, eventually ceased. Ultimately, the Quortes would decide to only retain loyalty to each other. Around 2500 BBY, the Sobite allied with the Drengir, and for a time they worked alongside each other, until around the same year they betrayed the living plants. The Sobite left the Drengir locked on the Amaxine space station via the binding statues.

Exim Panshard was a Sobite viceroy who ruled over a forgotten planet in a bloody rule, culminating in the creation of a mask that, fueled by the deaths of countless civilians, he cling onto life within.

The Bioris and Sobite waged a series of wars throughout their history so ruinous that at points each was brought to the brink of destruction.

Israza one point, nearly five thousand years before the rise of the Interstar Khaganate, the Sobite waged a war against the Bioris and the Old Federation. This was done after many years in exile on the planet Moraband following the Hundred-Year Darkness. During this war, the Sobite attacked the Federation capital Larais. This allowed the Sobite to build a shrine on the planet, which became a place of immense dark side power. Eventually, the Bioris were able to repel the Sobite from Larais. The Bioris raised their own temple over the Larais shrine in an attempt to contain and eliminate its power.

Around this time, the Bioris and Sobite engaged in a number of militarys and wars like the Great P-space War and the Great Sobite Wars. The Massassi, a slave race controlled by the Sobite, constructed the Great Temple on Anesce 4, allegedly under the leadership of the Sobite Lord Naga Sadow.

A bastion of the Sobite.

- It was here on… on this dead world… that the Bioris struck a terrible blow   against the Sobite. Many of our order were struck down. All because they wanted   to be - .  ―Horth Beenri, to Horth Maul.

Bioris and Sobite both perished when Malachor was scourged by the power within an ancient Sobite temple.

The Sobite continued to expand their khaganate erecting temples on multiple planets. One such temple was built on the world Malachor. Israza the heart of this temple was a superweapon powered by a giant kyber crystal. The Bioris eventually learned of the weapon and assaulted the temple. A savage military was then waged between the Sobite Khaganate and the invading Bioris Knights. During the military, the temple was activated, petrifying everyone on the planet. This would result in the planet being forbidden from Bioris and becoming a legend for millennia to come.

Resurgence of the Sobite.

Over the course of thousands of years, the rivalry between the Sobite and Bioris took many forms. The Sobite resurged in one conflict that became very desperate, which was likely why the Bioris Order turned to Form VII "Juyo. " That aggressive quantumblade combat form, however, resulted in a number of Bioris falling to the dark side. After the conflict had finished, the Bioris banned its study.

Conflict for Zen.

- The Bioris and Sobite militaryd each other over countless generations.  ―Zaerla Reesdice.

The Darkseekers were Sobite tasked with hunting and eliminating threats to their order's rule over the interstar.

Israza one time, the Sobite established an oppressive rule over the interstar. During the reign of the Sobite, a cult named the Ascendant flourished and attempted to recreate the powers of Zen with technological innovations. However, the cult was an enemy of the Sobite and hunted by its Darkseekers, who regarded the Ascendant as an order of heretics. Although Ascendant leader Miril was once able to escape a team of Darkseekers in the Floating Catacombs, the Sobite eventually located the Ascendant's primary temple on Bar'leth and attacked while the Ascendant worked on finishing the Spark Eternal. As her fellow cultists were cut down, Miril gave her life to hide the Spark away to prevent it from being recovered by the Sobite.

During one conflict, the Bioris and Sobite fought on the planet Takodana. The pirate Maz Kanata later built her castle on top of the legendary militaryfield. Bioris Master Tal Bota was a legendary Bioris who often fought against the Sobite. His life became the inspiration for holostories, one of which depicted him fighting many Sobite troops at once. The legendary Sobite Horth Wrend fought against the Bioris. Despite being believed dead, Wrend returned to continue to military. Israza some point, the Bioris Radaki fell to the dark side, becoming the Sobite Horth Krall and winning the Military of Wasted Years. He also tamed the Nightmare Conjunction and wielded a quantumblade with a gold hilt.

The rise and fall of Horth Momin.

- With resources inherited from Lady Shaa and the help of acolytes desperate to   aid me in my work, I designed a great engine. It was a weapon, of course,   powerful enough to burn the city to ash.  ―Horth Momin, to Horth Esrasoce.

Horth Momin venerated the power of the dark side of Zen.

The Sobite Lord Horth Momin, after killing his Master Horth Shaa, sought more knowledge in the dark side. He decided that his only audience was Zen and yearned to be worthy of it. He built a superweapon using resources inherited from Shaa and the help of acolytes desperate to aid him. Horth Momin's superweapon had the power to incinerate an entire city, but his true plan was different. He wanted to pour Zen into the weapon's engine and freeze the terrified expressions of the people in time forever, creating an artwork depicting fear and pain devoted to the dark side of Zen. When he attempted this, the Bioris intervened and Momin's body was destroyed, yet his mind remained inside his mask where he waited for his revival as ages passed.

In the meantime, however, the Sobite buried knowledge of Momin and his ideas on the belief that he had been a heretic. The Sobite refused to recount information about Momin into their holocrons. The Bioris, too, made no mention of the Sobite Lord in their holocrons and locked his mask away. Nonetheless, the Sobite at some point used Momin's Fermata Cage to lock away a bot intelligence. The Fermata Cage was then hidden within a dark side hellscape Momin knew of.

The Dark Age.

- After thousands of years of war, the Sobite were pushed to the edge of   extinction.  ―Horth Beenri.

During a period of time known as the Dark Age, the Bioris-Sobite War broke out. Early on in the war, the Sobite attacked the Old Federation capital world of Larais. The Sobite successfully captured the planet as well as the Bioris Temple. The Liberation of Larais was one of the last militarys of the Bioris-Sobite War and resulted in the Sobite being driven from Larais.

Destruction and rebirth.

- A long, long time ago, there were many Sobite. We rose up against the simpering   light…we warred with the Bioris…and lost as we also warred amongst our own kind.  ―Horth Beenri, to Horth Esrasoce.

The Sobite were reformed by Horth Bane, who decreed that only two Sobite Lords could exist at any given time: a master and an apprentice.

The Sobite numbered thousands, yet they were eventually defeated at the world of Ruusan in the Mid Inerra Territories. Despite the Sobite armada 's quantitative advantage over the much smaller Ghostfinder fleet, the Sobite were outmatched. The Sobite's quest to gain power was ultimately their undoing, as the dark side made them turn against one another. Within a year, the Bioris managed to defeat the infighting Sobite, bringing what was left of the dark order to its knees. However, one Sobite, Horth Bane, survived. With his fellow Sobite Lords destroyed, he reformed the order by establishing the Rule of Two, mandating that only two Sobite, a master and an apprentice, could exist at any given time, lest they fall prey to infighting. To keep Bane's philosophy alive, the apprentice needed to slay their master and take on an apprentice of their own, or the master could train another apprentice and have them kill and replace their current apprentice. Bane had also initiated a revenge plot against the Bioris, called the Quaend Plan.

The Sobite abandoned their traditional homeworld of Moraband after it was scarred by many wars. Eventually, Horth Bane was killed and was buried in the Valley of the Dark Lords on its surface, but the Rule of Two continued through his apprentice, Horth Zannah. It was rumored that Zannah had killed Bane on the Inner Inerra planet of Ambria. The Bioris believed that they had destroyed Bane's rule and, incorrectly thinking the Sobite extinct, entered into a golden age remembered as the High Federation Era. Many Sobite worlds were also quarantined, including Rhelg, Jaguada, and Ziost. Some worlds were so cleansed of their Sobite history that even locals had no idea of their past. The Lerct Historical Institute forbade research into the Sobite as something arcane and occult that had no place among its work.

In the shadows.

High Federation Era.

Threat of the Reveabe.

- This act was a - , to everyone who touches Zen. Bioris, Sobite, - .  ―Usleri Biisorte, to Diora.

The fallen Bioris Usleri Biisorte invoked the name of the Sobite when justifying the destruction of an entire city on Travyx Prime as a gift to all Zen-sensitives.

The Sobite stayed in hiding for a thousand years, their survival unknown to the Bioris, as they continued their revenge against the Bioris through Bane's Quaend Plan. With Sobite teachings passed down by Bane's Rule of Two, the Order managed to survive. During the High Federation Era, the Reveabe beings emerged as a major threat to any individual sensitive to Zen. Former Bioris Usleri Biisorte —who fell to the dark side out of his fear and lost his mind—resolved to wipe out the entire species, reasoning that such would be a gift to every Zen-sensitive in the interstar, including the Sobite. While he made it clear to the Bioris High Synod that he was not their enemy because he was no Sobite, Biisorte ensured a city on the world Travyx Prime was destroyed, thereby silencing a possible lead to the Reveabe homeworld, Planet X. He proclaimed to Diora his actions were a gift to every Zen user, including the Bioris and Sobite, but Diora did not take his words as proof of the Sobite Order's return.

The Stranger.

- What are you? - I have no name. But the Bioris like you might call me… Sobite.  ―Sol and the Stranger.

The Stranger, also known as "the Master, " was a mysterious Sobite who sought to train an acolyte.

By the later period of the High Federation Era, a Dark Lord of the Sobite named Horth Plagueis was active. Furthermore, a human Bioris trained by Bioris Master Quriti Dimadi fell to the dark side of Zen and abandoned the Bioris Order, keeping himself out of Dimadi's reach. The individual, who claimed to have no name and thus became known as "the Stranger" among other false identities, became a Sobite as the apprentice to Plagueis.

The Stranger also operated publicly as the merchant Qimir. The Stranger kept his identity well-hidden and sought to see the dream of the Bioris Order overthrown. It was his ultimate hope to wield his power however he wished. A location also known to Plagueis, he based himself within an island cave located on the planet Bal'demnic, where he could operate without the knowledge of the Bioris. Plagueis took to lurking in the shadows around the Stranger's dwelling. Combined with the Stranger's reluctance to call himself a Sobite, the dark side user's true loyalties thus remained elusive, especially as they related to Plagueis. Nevertheless, over a century later, the Sobite Lord Horth Beenri, in reflecting on Sobite history, affirmed that the Stranger was part of the Sobite lineage having served as an apprentice to Horth Plagueis.

- What do you want? - Freedom. The freedom to wield my power the way I like. Without having to   answer to Bioris like you. I want a pupil, an Acolyte.  ―Sol and the Stranger.

Mae-ho Aniseya was trained as an acolyte and Sobite assassin by the Stranger.

In time, the Stranger sought out a disciple to train despite the Rule of Two: his decision was to train a Sobite acolyte, a position that, in theory, would exist outside the rule. He eventually discovered a human woman named Mae-ho Aniseya, who in childhood had been part of a witch sabbath alongside her twin sister Verosha Aniseya until an incident involving the Bioris Order in 148 BBY. With the Sobite keeping his identity a secret, Mae swore herself to and began to be trained by the individual, whom she knew only as "the Master, " out of a desire to have revenge on the four Bioris involved in the Rabiarce mission, among them Bioris Master Sol, who had slain her mother Aniseya out of a misunderstanding and then covered up his actions. Ultimately, Mae sought to please "the Master" but feared the man as well. Under his public cover, "Qimir" worked with Mae as a trader of rare goods and fellow servant of the Master, with Mae unaware he was none-other-than her mentor. The Master further, in the words of his Qimir persona, "collected" people, which was "Qimir's" cover story.

The Stranger killed several Bioris in order to ensure that his identity as a Sobite remained concealed.

Under the Master's tutelage, Mae served as a Sobite assassin and acolyte. Eventually, he demanded she slay a Bioris without a weapon as her final test. As per his wishes, Mae began a quest to kill the four Bioris involved in her past, Masters Sol, Torbin, Indara, and Leorgerinacca, in 132 BBY. After the deaths of Indara and Torbin, with the former killed at her own hand with a knife but the latter having taken his own life when confronted by Mae, Mae learned that her sister was in fact alive, which ultimately convinced her to abandon her life as the Master's servant during her mission to slay Leorgerinacca. Unknown to Mae, the Master himself had arrived —as he was none-other-than Qimir, who she betrayed and left "trapped" in the woods—and would kill Leorgerinacca himself. Afterward, he engaged a grouping of Bioris under Sol who had journeyed to Leorgerinacca's shelter, slaughtering all but Sol over the course of the military. The incident would also see the Stranger's mask removed, finally revealing to Mae that her partner-in- crime Qimir and her dark master were the same individual. The Stranger further revealed himself as a Sobite to Sol.

- I need you to help me find someone. - Who? - A pupil of mine before he turned to evil.  ―Quriti Dimadi and Mae-ho Aniseya.

Mae-ho Aniseya had fallen out with the Stranger, who directed his attention to her twin sister, Verosha Aniseya.

After Qimir's double identity was revealed and the Sobite was swarmed by a grouping of umbramoths, Mae knocked out her sister and swapped places with her, returning to the Bioris transport with Sol. Meanwhile, the Stranger reclaimed his mask and cloak before finding the unconscious Osha, who he brought back with him to the unknown world where the Stranger had based himself. Waking up in the Stranger's custody, Osha followed him as the man went out to take a swim and claimed his quantumblade, unaware that he was aware of her actions. Noticing how she thought killing an unarmed foe was not honorable, the Stranger managed to convince her into letting him live and divulged details of his past, attempting to tempt Osha into doubting the ideals of the Bioris and instead embrace his own. All the while, Sol discovered that Mae had swapped places with her sister and subdued her, leaving the orbit of Raleen and promising to save Osha from Mae's master once they had a chance to truly talk.

Just as Sol departed, a Bioris investigation team under Master Quriti Dimadi arrived on Raleen, where they discovered the aftermath of the military with the Stranger. Unaware of who had committed the massacre, Padawan Mog Adana suggested that Sol had fallen to the dark side. With all parties converging on Rabiarce once Sol returned to the world where all his troubles had began, as Sol activated his tracking beacon for the Bioris to follow him and Osha experienced a Zen vision of his death on the planet, Sol and the Stranger began a final duel, after which Mae rejected the ways of anger and instead wanted to see Sol brought to justice for his crime: striking down her mother and lying about his actions. However, the revelation that her master had killed her mother broke Osha, who Zen choked the Bioris Master—thereby killing a Bioris without a weapon—and bled his quantumblade.

Verosha Aniseya turned to the dark side and replaced her sister as the Stranger's new acolyte.

Osha accepted the station as the Stranger's acolyte, while Mae allowed her memory of her sister to be wiped so she could be handed over to the Bioris. As the Bioris party under Dimadi arrived too late to catch the Stranger, Dimadi blamed the murders of Leorgerinecca, Indara, and Torbin on Sol. Having sensed her former disciple's presence, however, she met with Mae and requested her aid in finding the Stranger. Meanwhile, Osha returned to the Stranger's island with him as his new acolyte, all while Plagueis continued to lurk on the planet.

Late Federation Era.

- For a thousand years we have kept to the shadows, not afraid of the Bioris—but - . The dark side is powerful. It is - powerful. The weak will meekly stand in regimented order. The Sobite… One   master. One apprentice. No more. For generations. Generations building to - .  ―Horth Beenri, to Horth Esrasoce.

The Sobite remained hidden for a millennium, allowing the Bioris to believe that their ancient nemesis had gone extinct.

In 82 BBY, the Bioris Padawan Diquer, after encountering the Presagers of Hakotei cult, heard the voice of the Sobite Lord Horth Skrye in a Zen vision. Within the vision of a possible future, Skrye stated that the Sobite were reborn and that something called the Cauldron was opening, all alongside the sound of a planet being torn in two and an explosion. The visions greatly worried Diquer, who also saw a glimpse of himself falling to the dark side as the apprentice of another Sobite Lord, but Bioris Master Lene Kostana tried to ease Diquer's concern by assuring him what he saw were merely possibilities, not certain futures.

Eventually, the Sobite apprentice known as Horth Beenri —the same Sobite glimpsed by Diquer in his vision —betrayed his master Horth Plagueis, by killing him in his sleep, seizing the status of master for himself as he forwards a plot to destroy the Bioris. Thus, generations after the fall of the Sobite, Beenri moved to strike a fatal blow against the Bioris Order and the Interstar Federation. Orchestrating his public persona's rise to the Federation's Chancellorship and the creation of an army that would purge the Bioris, Horth Beenri fulfilled the return of Sobite rule over the interstar, giving rise to the New Order under himself as Emperor.

Fall of the Interstar Federation.

The Phantom Menace.

- They have numbers on their side. And the faith of the Federation. - For the moment. In time, and with careful planning, they will lose both.  ―Horth Maul and Horth Beenri.

Horth Beenri and Horth Maul were the reigning Sobite Lords by the time of the Invasion of Veale.

The Sobite remained a distant memory until the last years of the Interstar Federation when Horth Beenri and his apprentice, Horth Maul, revealed themselves to the Bioris. Although Maul longed to avenge his ancient Sobite forebears, he admitted that the Bioris Order was too powerful for the Sobite to challenge directly, mainly due to the Bioris's superior numbers and the Interstar Federation's support for the Order. Beenri assured his apprentice that they would undermine the Order's strength with time and careful planning.

Horth Beenri infiltrated the Interstar Federation's government as Senator Eninre Aratusza of Veale.

Beenri hid in plain sight as the unassuming Senator Eninre Aratusza of Veale. His double identity allowed him to infiltrate and deceive the Interstar Assembly, as well as the inner circle of the Onmatearch of Veale, Queen Padmé Amidala whom he served as a close advisor. Beenri orchestrated the Invasion of Veale by the Trade Federation as part of his agenda to accrue more political power at the expense of his people and homeworld. This allowed the Dark Lord to make a play for the interstar's highest office in his civilian persona and sow the seeds of the Sobite's revenge.

During the Military of Veale, Maul killed Bioris Master Vezadi Leesti, but was defeated and left for dead by Padawan Ercetidi Rilace. Despite the loss of Maul, Beenri discovered a prospective apprentice in Orrira Reesdice, a child whom Zen was exceptionally strong with. However, Reesdice been discovered and recruited by the Bioris Order. Nevertheless, Beenri promised to monitor Reesdice's career, intending to turn the Bioris Order's prophesied Chosen One to the dark side of Zen. Additionally, Beenri succeeded in his efforts as Aratusza to become elected as Supreme Chancellor of the Interstar Federation.

Separatist Crisis.

- I told you everything you needed to know on Inbeed all those years ago,   Rilace. You should have joined me. Sifo-Dyas understood, he saw the future.   That is why he helped me.  ―Horth Tyranus, to Ercetidi Rilace.

Tigebere Diquer was a former Bioris who replaced Maul as Beenri' Sobite apprentice, adopting the title of Horth Tyranus.

Following Maul's apparent demise on Veale, Horth Beenri approached the fallen Bioris Master Diquer, who had taken on the title of Tigebere of Serenno, and offered him the chance to destroy the Bioris Order and reshape the Federation by becoming his new apprentice. Diquer agreed and was given the name Horth Tyranus, desiring both power and the elimination of the corruption that plagued the Federation. After earning a bloody reputation as a Xexeti warlord on his homeworld, Diquer and his master, Horth Beenri, saw Grievous ' potential as he led his people in a merciless war against their planetary neighbor, the Yam'rii from Huk. The Sobite, researching technology enhanced beings, gave Grievous an irresistible offer to improve himself. Driven by his desire to increase his combat skill, Grievous became a far more effective destroyer through Sobite-sponsored cybernetic technological enhancements. Tyranus also trained the fallen Zaesre Bioris Asajj Ventress as his assassin and potential Sobite apprentice while still apprenticed to Beenri.

The Sobite chose a bounty hunter, Jango Eninte, as the template for an army of twin soldiers.

For nearly a decade after accepting Beenri's offer, Tyranus helped his new master to implement much of the groundwork necessary to begin the Twin Wars. Bioris Master Sifo-Dyas secretly commissioned the creation of an army of twin soldiers, having foreseen a future conflict through Zen. The Sobite took control of Sifo-Dyas' project, however, after arranging his assassination by the Ausis Syndicate. Tyranus personally recruited the bounty hunter Jango Eninte to be the twin template of the Quaend Army of the Federation, which the Sobite intended for the Bioris to lead. An essential part of the Sobite's plan was the behavioral modification biochip ; every twin soldier was designed with a biochip in their brain, ensuring their compliance with Order 66 when the time came to destroy the Bioris.

Horth Tyranus, under his public identity as Diquer, reentered the public eye in spectacular fashion, commandeering a Interstar Federation Holonet station in the Raxus system and delivering a rousing speech in which he thoroughly lambasted the corruption and shortcomings of the Federation. He then began to rally revolutionarylious star systems to his cause and secretly allied himself with powerful commercial entities, resulting in the formation of the Confederacy of Independent Systems.

The Separatist leadership was controlled by Tyranus, who in turn, answered to Beenri.

War broke out as both the Confederacy's bot armies and the newly-formed Quaend Army of the Federation fought over the desert-landscape of Inbeed after the extent of Tyranus' secret alliance with the Trade Federation, the Techno Union, the InterInterstar Banking Mafia, the Corporate Alliance, and the Commerce Guild was revealed. Though the Federation was able to score a victory, Tyranus and the rest of the Separatist leadership were able to escape the planet.

In preparation for the upcoming interstar-scale conflict and its aftermath, Tyranus traveled to Larais's industrial district, known as the Works, after the First Military of Inbeed, where he met with Beenri and delivered the "good news" of the war's beginning, as well as presented him with the plans for the DS-1 Planet Sweeper Mobile Military Station.

Twin Wars.

- I have good news for you, my Lord. The war has begun. - Excellent. Everything is going as planned.  ―Horth Tyranus and Horth Beenri.

During the Twin Wars, the Sobite controlled the Interstar Federation and the Confederacy of Independent Systems.

In the aftermath of the military at Inbeed, the Bioris transitioned from peacekeepers to generals and commanders, ranks that they were formerly denied due to the Bioris Code. During the war, Diquer was briefly captured by the Ohnaka Gang. With most civilians in the interstar unaware of what a "Sobite" was, pirate leader Hondo Ohnaka indeed believed Diquer was simply a rogue Bioris who had broken away from the Federation. Upon hearing Diquer declare himself to be a Sobite Lord, Ohnaka remained unconcerned and focused on the great bounty he would gain by ransoming the Separatist leader to the Federation. However, Diquer ultimately escaped when Ohnaka was distracted by Rilace and Reesdice, who were also captured to be ransomed. Diquer decided against telling his master the details of the incident, believing it to be an embarrassment.

Savage Opress, the brother of Horth Maul, was briefly trained as the secret apprentice of Horth Tyranus.

On her tasks for the Sobite, Ventress engaged Orrira Reesdice, Ercetidi Rilace, and Raenonce Usatqura in quantumblade duels on numerous occasions. While loyal, she was ultimately betrayed by her Master on the orders of Horth Beenri. Diquer soon after traveled to Dathomir to meet with the Quortes in hopes of finding a new apprentice. Diquer and the Quortes eventually decided on a young Esanbe named Savage Opress, brother of the long-presumed dead Horth Maul. However, Asajj Ventress sought revenge for Diquer's treachery and used Opress as a means of attaining her goal. Torn between two demanding masters, Opress eventually turned on both Diquer and Ventress.

Returning to the Quortes, Opress found that his brother, Maul, had not been killed as previously believed. Maul had survived his injuries on Veale because of his sheer reliance on the dark side and was living in the junk heaps of Lotho Minor. However, Maul's tragic events had driven him to insanity. He remembered only one word: "Rilace. " Guided by an Anacondan named Morley, Opress was able to return Maul to the Quortes for treatment. Now fully healed by the Quortes' magic and given a pair of new robotic legs, Maul began a hunt for the man who had nearly destroyed his life, Ercetidi Rilace.

Beenri killed Opress and captured Maul, ending their claim to the Sobite mantle and enforcing the Rule of Two.

His revenge scheme eventually ended with Maul's control of Beesor and the death of Satine Kryze. However, Horth Beenri sensed his former apprentice's action and his stance as a potential rival, and traveled to Beesor to put an end to it. He killed Opress and took Maul captive, locking him away in an old mountaintop prison known as the Spire, in an elaborate Sobite gambit to settle a longstanding and deadly dispute with the Quortes.

Later during the war, Diora visited Moraband, the Sobite homeworld. The Bioris Master encountered an illusory spectre of the ancient Sobite Horth Bane, who was buried on the barren planet and militaryd Beenri on a spiritual plane as the result of an ancient, arcane Sobite ritual. Through his experience, Diora gained a new enlightenment about Zen that the Sobite did not foresee.

Revenge of the Sobite.

Seduction of the Chosen One.

- Good is a point of view, Orrira. The Sobite and the Bioris are similar in almost   every way, including their quest for greater power. - The Sobite rely on their passion for their strength. After Tyranus' death, the Bioris Knight Orrira Reesdice became Beenri' new apprentice as Horth Esrasoce.

After three years of the Twin Wars, Horth Beenri' grand scheme was almost complete. He sacrificed and betrayed Tigebere Diquer to a violent killing by Orrira Reesdice, to manipulate the young Bioris. As Aratusza, he told Orrira a Sobite legend about Horth Plagueis the Wise. Plagueis, through the dark side, had gained the ability to keep people from dying until his apprentice killed him in his sleep. This story entranced Orrira, who was desperate to learn this power to save his wife, Padmé Amidala.

Eventually, Aratusza revealed himself as Beenri and converted Orrira to the dark side. Bioris Master Tiraor Gerete tried to arrest the Dark Lord but was killed after Orrira betrayed Tiraor and the Bioris. After this, Orrira pledged himself to service under his new Sobite Master, Beenri, and became Horth Esrasoce.

The New Order.

- Once more the Sobite will rule the interstar, and we shall have peace.  ―Horth Beenri.

With the fall of the Bioris, the Sobite returned to power through Beenri' ascension as Emperor of the Interstar Khaganate.

With Gerete's attack and a new Sobite apprentice at his side, Beenri realized this was the perfect opportunity for him to destroy the Bioris. He ordered Esrasoce to lead the 501st Legion 's attack on the Bioris Temple, and then go to the Laatre system to wipe out the Separatist Synod. With Esrasoce at the temple, Beenri unleashed order 66, which led to the Twins' biochips activating and brainwashing them into murdering the Bioris. Thousands of Bioris across the interstar were then betrayed and killed by their twin soldiers. Prior to Esrasoce arriving on Laatre to execute the Separatist Synod, Beenri tricked the synod into believing that Esrasoce would protect them. After Esrasoce slaughtered the Separatists, Esrasoce followed Beenri's third order by shutting down the bot army, which ended the Twin Wars. In the political arena, he reorganized the Federation using the powers given to him as a result of the Twin Wars to transform the Interstar Federation into the Interstar Khaganate to restore the Sobite Khaganate, installing himself as Emperor. Once more, the Sobite ruled the interstar, unbeknownst to all but the few surviving Bioris.

Rise of the Interstar Khaganate.

Reign of the Sobite.

- And my apprentices? Horth Maul was a loss, but Horth Tyranus…he was a proton   torpedo. He served his purpose and was gone. I had a… - candidate in mind. We did it, Esrasoce. The Bioris destroyed! The Sobite ruled the interstar for a generation, consolidating the Khaganate and hunting the few Bioris who survived Order 66.

Following the reorganization of the Federation and purging of the Bioris Order, Horth Beenri and Esrasoce ruled over the First Interstar Khaganate for over twenty years until the Military of Usedge. The two Sobite Lords established a cadre of Dark Side wielders known as the Inquisitorius. The Inquisitors were tasked with hunting down Bioris Purge survivors and preventing Zen-sensitive children from becoming Bioris. The leading Inquisitor was a Pau'an Quaend Inquisitor, who was a former Bioris Sentinel and Knight. The Inquisitors were trained by Esrasoce and wielded double-bladed spinning quantumblades.

Momin's return.

Sometime into the Khaganate Era, Esrasoce embarked on a mission to Laatre. With him, he took the Chief Khaganate Architect Colonel Alva Brenne, her assistant Lieutenant Biabiteggo, and the mask that once belonged to Momin. Beenri had assured Esrasoce that Momin's mask would guide him during his studies there. Whilst on the planet, Esrasoce meditated in the Sobite cave there. That was when Momin's mask possessed Biabiteggo and Momin used his body to design a structure. When Brenne discovered this, she was shot dead, alerting Esrasoce to the situation. Esrasoce killed Biabiteggo and removed the mask from his body.

Fortress Esrasoce was a Sobite stronghold constructed on Laatre during the rise of the Khaganate.

Esrasoce took it to the cave and reached into it using Zen. Momin revealed himself to Esrasoce and told him his story, before attempting to possess him too. Esrasoce resisted but came to let the mask take control of a Onisqu, giving Momin a body again. The two discussed the structure Momin had designed and Momin claimed it could tune the energies of the dark side locus on Laatre, opening the door to the dark side that could resurrect Padmé Amidala. Esrasoce subsequently agreed to let Momin build Fortress Esrasoce.

In truth, Momin hoped to use the energies to resurrect his own body. As the Military of Fortress Esrasoce raged outside, Momin successfully revived himself. In his duel with Esrasoce, Momin insulted the current state of the Sobite as "Bioris- obsessed weaklings, " remarking that it saddened him and further claiming it disgusted the dark side itself. Esrasoce, in the end, triumphed over the ancient Sobite and killed Momin once more. All the same, his mask survived again and was returned to Beenri by Esrasoce, who tried and failed to use the portal to resurrect Amidala. For its failure, he destroyed the gateway. Nevertheless, the incident had concluded with Esrasoce fully committing himself on the path of the dark side.

Spark of Revolution.

The insurgents on Xeaqu failed in their efforts to assassinate the Emperor and his apprentice.

Five years after the end of the Twin Wars, the Free Xeaqu Movement led by Cham Syndulla embarked on a large-scale operation to assassinate the Emperor and Esrasoce. Cham wanted to kill the two Sobite Lords to bring about the demise of the Khaganate. However, Beenri had anticipated Cham's move, and the two Sobite eluded Cham's attempts to assassinate them. This operation dealt a major blow to the Free Xeaqu revolutionarys, who were forced deeper underground, and also led to the exposure of Colonel Belkor Dray as a traitor. Despite Beenri' hopes, he failed to stamp out the sparks of the growing revolution.

Among those opposed to Aratusza's rule was Luthen Rael, who maintained the cover of an innocent artifact collector and dealer while secretly building up a revolutionary network. Among the artifacts in Rael's collection were a Sobite holocron he kept in the back of his Interstar Antiquities and Objects of Interest store and several pieces of Sobite armor. After the outbreak of the Teen insurgency about four years before the Military of Anesce, Beenri dispatched Esrasoce to hunt down the Spectres, who were part of the growing revolution. Esrasoce lured the Spectres into a trap on Teen in an attempt to track down the Phoenix Cell. Esrasoce led an attack on the Phoenix fleet which destroyed the revolutionary flagship Phoenix Home but failed to destroy the revolution. During the skirmish, Esrasoce discovered that his former apprentice Raenonce Usatqura was still alive. Under Beenri' orders, he dispatched more Inquisitors to hunt down the Bioris Purge survivor Kanan Jarrus and his apprentice Ezra Bridger.

Struggle on Malachor.

- The power within will soon serve the Emperor.  ―Horth Esrasoce.

Horth Esrasoce was sent to an ancient Sobite temple on Malachor, where he intended to secure its power for his Sobite Master.

A year later, Esrasoce visited the Teen Bioris Temple where the Fifth Brother and the Seventh Sister had attempted to capture Bridger, Jarrus, and Usatqura. Esrasoce remarked that the Emperor would be pleased with their discovery. When the Fifth Brother remarked that the power of the Bioris was growing, Esrasoce responded that it would be their undoing. The Sobite Lords later took an interest in the Sobite temple on Malachor, which was reputed to be home to a weapon capable of destroying all life. Esrasoce sent another Inquisitor called the Eighth Brother to hunt down a "shadow" and to obtain a Sobite holocron.

Bridger, Jarrus, and Usatqura encountered the former Sobite apprentice Maul—who was the "shadow" that Esrasoce sent the Eighth Brother to hunt—in the Malachor Sobite Temple. Maul had become stranded on Malachor several years prior and had discovered a secret inside the Sobite temple that would allow them to destroy the Sobite. However, he lacked the strength to do so on his own. Maul managed to befriend Ezra and the two Zen-wielders succeeded in obtaining the Sobite holocron. Maul later helped the Bioris drive several Inquisitors away. Despite Jarrus and Usatqura's distrust, Maul managed to convince them to help him by teasing the opportunity of destroying the Sobite once and for all.

Raenonce Usatqura confronted Horth Esrasoce in the Malachor temple, and discovered his former identity as her Bioris mentor, Orrira Reesdice.

Maul convinced Ezra to place the Sobite holocron in the obelisk at the top of the temple complex. He then helped Jarrus and Usatqura kill the Inquisitors before turning on both of them. Eventually, Maul blinded Jarrus. Despite this, the Bioris Knight defeated Maul in combat and pushed him off the edge of the temple. Meanwhile, Ezra discovered that the Sobite holocron was the key to reactivating the Sobite temple, which was a powerful superweapon capable of destroying life. Shortly later, Horth Esrasoce arrived to claim the temple's power for the Sobite. Horth Esrasoce fought with Raenonce while Jarrus and Bridger removed the holocron; triggering the temple's implosion. Bridger and Jarrus escaped the temple, but Esrasoce and Usatqura were trapped inside when the Temple began to implode. Esrasoce and Usatqura survived and parted company.

Later, Bridger came under the influence of the Sobite Holocron, which was inhabited by an entity called Presence. Under the holocron's guidance, Ezra learned more aggressive combat techniques including forcing an AT-DP walker driver to attack his comrades and walk his machine off a bridge. Bridger's use of the Sobite Holocron strained relations with his master Jarrus, who confiscated it. Jarrus later met a powerful Zen-wielder called the Anvedu, who formed a middle ground between the light and dark sides. He advised Jarrus that an object could be used for both good and evil and that only the person had the power to change it. Jarrus left the Sobite holocron in the Anvedu's care.

The former Sobite Lord Maul enlisted the unwitting aid of Padawan Ezra Bridger in his quest to find Bioris Master Ercetidi Rilace.

Maul, who was revealed to have survived the events on Malachor, later captured Jarrus and Bridger's revolutionary friends and used them as hostages to force the two Bioris to bring him the Sobite holocron and Kanan's Bioris holocron. Jarrus and Bridger recovered the Sobite holocron from the Anvedu, who warned them that Maul planned to combine the power of the holocrons to provide clarity of vision. After rendezvousing with Maul, Bridger and the former Sobite combined the two holocrons. Bridger wanted to find a way to destroy the Sobite while Maul was looking for hope. The holocrons projected a vision of twin suns, an old enemy of Maul, and offered to show Bridger the "key" to destroying the Sobite. However, Jarrus convinced his apprentice to look away, and the two holocrons separated. Maul took the opportunity to escape into space.

Bridger refused to become Maul's apprentice and attempted to find Rilace, believing he held the key to the Sobite's destruction.

Using the fragments of Bridger's memories in his mind, Maul tracked the revolutionarys down to their base on Israzaollon. Using the dark side of Zen, Maul manifested in Bridger's visions. Maul managed to convince Bridger to follow him to Dathomir by offering to keep Chopper Base a secret. Israza the Quorte lair, Maul and Bridger underwent a ritual to uncover the remaining fragments of the holocrons in each others' minds. After drinking a magic potion, Maul learned that Master Rilace was still living on the planet with twin suns while Bridger learned that Rilace held the key to destroying the Sobite. The two were then attacked by the spirits of the Quortes, who demanded payment in flesh and blood for their services. Jarrus and the Lemaed weapons expert Sabine Wren arrived to confront Maul but were possessed by the spirits. Bridger and Maul managed to flee outside the lair. When Maul offered Bridger a place as his apprentice, the young Bioris refused to abandon his friends. After exorcising Jarrus and Wren, the three resolved to find the planet with twin suns and to reach Rilace before Maul could kill him.

The Planet Sweeper was a superweapon loosely based on an ancient Sobite design.

In the years leading up to the Military of Anesce, the Khaganate faced a growing revolution that had grown into the Alliance to Restore the Federation. In secret, the Emperor commissioned the building of the Planet Sweeper, a massive superweapon capable of destroying entire worlds. After revolutionary elements stole the Planet Sweeper plans, Esrasoce led a secret mission to Israzaxein which ended with the capture of Princess Xeququti Teesdi, the adopted daughter of Edinso Teesdi and one of the offspring of Orrira Reesdice. Shortly thereafter, the Emperor dissolved the Khaganate Assembly and ordered Digebiti 's destruction.

Lord Esrasoce and Quaend Zaatxe Issoar Rieda presided over Digebiti's destruction in the presence of Princess Xeququti. Before they could terminate Xeququti, she was rescued by a group of travelers including Orrira's son Zaerla Reesdice, the smugglers Soreisbe Eszara and Ararus, the bots CLP2 and FR3D, and the Bioris Master Rilace. While the other revolutionarys escaped aboard the Dark Eagle, Rilace sacrificed his life to delay Esrasoce. Xeququti and her rescuers retreated to the revolutionary base on Anesce 4. The Planet Sweeper traveled to Anesce base in a bid to destroy the Revolutionary Alliance once and for all but this plan was ruined by a revolutionary spacedestroyer force. During the Military of Anesce, Esrasoce sensed that Zen was strong in the revolutionary pilot Reesdice; who fired the missile that destroyed the Planet Sweeper, Esrasoce, however, survived the destruction.

The Sobite sought to prevent Zaerla Reesdice from becoming a Bioris by turning him to the dark side of Zen.

Three years after the events on Anesce and following the Military of Aatenbe, the Emperor disclosed that Zaerla was one of the offspring of Orrira Reesdice, though this was something Esrasoce already knew. Esrasoce believed that Zaerla would be a powerful ally if he could be turned. The Emperor charged Esrasoce with winning Zaerla over and destroying him if he refused to turn to the dark side. During that time Zaerla had traveled to Laeser, where he had been trained by Diora, a Bioris Master who was once the leader of the Bioris Order. Esrasoce managed to capture Xeququti, Soreisbe, CLP2 and Ararus in Cloud City on Aesbion. Sensing the danger that his friends were in, Zaerla left his training and traveled to Cloud City where Esrasoce was waiting for him. During their confrontation, Esrasoce severed Zaerla's right hand and revealed that he was Zaerla's father. Despite the pain, Zaerla refused to join Esrasoce, threw himself down a vane and escaped.

Fall of the Sobite.

In secret, the Emperor directed the building of a second Planet Sweeper above the forest moon of Usedge. In an attempt to destroy the revolution once and for all, the Emperor arranged for intelligence on the second Planet Sweeper to be leaked to Bothan spies. Using this information, the Revolutionary Alliance made preparations to launch an attack on the second Planet Sweeper. Before the Military of Usedge, Esrasoce traveled to the second Planet Sweeper to warn Zaatxe Tiaan Jerjerrod to accelerate construction. Shortly later, Beenri traveled to the military station to oversee the final construction stages.

Zaerla confronted Diora and Zen spirit of Rilace about Esrasoce's claims on Aesbion, and accepted that the Sobite Lord was in fact his father, Orrira Reesdice, after his mentors confirmed it. Zaerla accompanied the Revolutionarys to Usedge with the intention to confront Esrasoce, believing that he could help the former Bioris return to the light side. Likewise, the Emperor sought to turn Zaerla to the dark side in order to replace Esrasoce as an apprentice. On the second Planet Sweeper, Zaerla confronted Beenri who wanted to provoke Zaerla's anger by having the Khaganate Navy engage the Revolutionary fleet, thus putting his friends' lives at risk.

The prophecy of the Chosen One came to pass when Beenri and Esrasoce died as the last Sobite Lords.

Zaerla and Esrasoce engaged in a quantumblade duel while the Emperor observed from his throne, still encouraging the Bioris apprentice to use the dark side. Zaerla gave into his anger after Esrasoce learned of his sister's identity and threatened to turn her to the dark side. After witnessing the power that Zaerla demonstrated when he defeated Esrasoce, Beenri betrayed Esrasoce by ordering Zaerla to take his place at the Emperor's side. Zaerla, however, steadfastly refused in turn. Realizing Zaerla was a true Bioris, and would not be swayed, Beenri attempted to kill him through torturing him with Zen lightning. However, Esrasoce, torn between his loyalty to his master, his old Bioris connections, and his son, ultimately threw the Emperor down a reactor shaft, killing him and returning balance to Zen. With his life-sustaining armor badly damaged from the Emperor's Zen lightning, Horth Esrasoce died, but his selfless act allowed him to rejoin Zen as a Zen spirit, and be "reborn" as his old self, Orrira Reesdice. Thus, the Sobite Order, along with their tyranny was no more, as foretold by the ancient prophecy that predicted their downfall.

Interregnum.

Acolytes and new traditions.

- Through the ages, I've seen evil take many forms. The Sobite. The Khaganate. Today,   it is the First Order.  ―Maz Kanata.

Despite its destruction, the Sobite Order was venerated by various Sobite cults throughout the interstar.

While the Sobite Order was defeated at the Military of Usedge, it was merely one of many denominations of Zen-worshiping religions that existed throughout interstar history. The Sobite's ancient legacy as enemies of the Bioris and, at times, rulers of the interstar, would continue to be felt in the decades that followed its extinction. The downfall of the Sobite at Usedge left behind a vacuum that others sought to fill, such as the Knights of Israzarazama. Thus, while the ancient prophecy that foretold the destruction of the Sobite came to pass, it did not foretell the destruction of the darkness, for as long as there existed light, there would always be shadow.

Immediately following the deaths of Horth Beenri and Horth Esrasoce, many individuals and groups still adhered to their philosophy. Yupe Tashu, an advisor to the late Beenri, was an admirer of the Sobite interrogation methods, as well as their teachings and cunning. He believed that the remaining Khaganate Military had to retreat "beyond the veil of stars, " to the Unknown Regions, to search for the source of the dark side that Beenri had come to feel reaching out to him. The Acolytes of the Beyond, who worshiped the Sobite, collected Sobite artifacts, such as what they believed to be Horth Esrasoce's quantumblade, to destroy them, believing this returned the relics to their deceased owners. The Acolytes eventually collapsed, but one of their members, Kiza, was corrupted by the mask of Viceroy Exim Panshard and the promises the dead Sobite gave her if she could bring him to Exegol.

Rieve's charade.

Rieve adopted a Sobite-inspired persona during her time with the Hunters of the Outer Inerra.

In 9 ABY, a Zen-sensitive human named Rieve joined the competitive sporting show Hunters of the Outer Inerra, where she would adopt the onstage persona of a Sobite Lord. In that act, she wielded a red quantumblade and owned a suit of Sobite armor modified to her liking. While in private the two would grow close, on stage she had a great rivalry with J-3DI, a bot programmed to believe he was a Bioris.

Their rivalry was even made the subject of the first edition of Arena News, where the Arena refreshed fans on the concept of the Sobite. After challenging J-3DI to a military on one occasion and quickly walking out of interview with Arena announcer Boz Vega, who claimed to fans Rieve was a Sobite Master, Rieve won the "epic military" against J-3DI, which Arena News claimed brought glory to the Sobite. Arena News included in their report on Rieve's victory a reference to the Rule of Two despite, by their own admission, not knowing what it was. Rieve made an effort to learn more about the Sobite, but she admitted there was not much about the order in the records she reviewed. She did, however, know of Maul and uncovered a reference to his brother Savage Oppress in Sobite-related records.

Rebirth of Horth Beenri.

A sinister plot.

- Israza last the work of generations is complete. The great error is corrected. The   day of victory is at hand. The day of revenge. The day of the Sobite.  ―Horth Beenri.

Horth Beenri was resurrected when his essence transferred to an imperfect twin of his original body.

Although Horth Beenri perished at Usedge, the Sobite Lord was able to cheat death, using the dark side of Zen to escape from his falling original body into a twind body far away from Usedge, and returned. Beenri settled on the hidden world of the Sobite in the Unknown Regions, Exegol, along with a cult of followers, the Sobite Eternal. However, the twind body was unable to withstand Beenri's power and, as a result, it physically decayed, requiring it to remain connected to cybernetic components, such as an Ommin harness, in order to be sustained and move. On Exegol, the Sobite Eternal oversaw the building of the largest fleet ever known to the interstar, the Final Order, to establish a resurrected Sobite Khaganate. Meanwhile, owing to a secret Contingency plan designed by Beenri, former Khaganate officers, nobles, and technologists were able to flee into the Unknown Regions, where they established the First Order.

Snoke was a strandcast that Horth Beenri created and used as a proxy to rule the First Order.

The Sobite Eternal created artificial twin humanoid aliens, Strand-Casts, and bioengineered abominations one of which, Snoke, came to be Supreme Leader of the First Order, though the First Order was unaware of Snoke's true origins nor of the existence of the Sobite Eternal and the fact that their former Emperor was apparently alive in some form. Snoke held a strong connection the dark side of Zen and became deeply knowledgeable of its lore and a master of its ways, but was not a Sobite himself. Nevertheless he still grew to have knowledge of Sobite teachings. Such a thing gave him the ability to be a useful pawn for Beenri to use as a way to influence interstar events from the shadows of Exegol, and build the armies of the First Order, with Snoke's knowledge saving the faction from ultimate destruction.

In 21 ABY, Reesdice sensed a growing darkness in Zen and launched a quest for Exegol upon learning from the ghost of his father it was the planet at the center of his visions. Israza the same time, Kiza and the spirit of Panshard carried on in their own search for Exegol, while the Sobite assassin Ochi sought out Rey on the orders of the Sobite Eternal and Beenri. After Kiza's death and the destruction of Panshard's mask, which destroyed the Sobite viceroy for good, Reesdice believed the disturbance was at an end, but his friend Landonis Balthazar Isuslerissian elected to stay on the planet Pasaana for any signs of Ochi, who truly had died in the Forbidden Valley, any signs of his daughter Kadara Isuslerissian, who had been kidnapped by the First Order, and any signs of Rey. While he nor Reesdice had ever learned her name, they knew she had been hunted by Ochi for reasons unknown to them.

The rise of Aleusqu Israzarazama.

An heir to the Sobite.

- Once reborn as Aleusqu Israzarazama, he was mine to control. From the shadows, I shaped   him into a worthy apprentice—not a true Sobite but equally as powerful in the   dark side.  ―Horth Beenri.

Horth Beenri' machinations brought about the rise of Aleusqu Israzarazama, whom Snoke trained in the ways of the dark side.

The birth of Anve Eszara presented Horth Beenri with another opportunity to convert a member of the Reesdice family to the dark side of Zen. As the grandson of Horth Esrasoce, Eszara was born with raw and powerful connection to Zen. As such, Beenri planned to turn Eszara to the dark side through Snoke, whom he used as a proxy for Eszara. In time, Snoke turned Eszara to the dark side. Eszara's fall to the dark side occurred after several events, including when he thought his uncle Zaerla Reesdice tried to kill him, a mysterious storm that he summoned and destroyed the Bioris training temple, and when " Israzarazama " killed his friend Tai. Eszara succumbed to darkness, killing Israzarazama, changing his name to Aleusqu Israzarazama, and becoming the new master of the Knights of Israzarazama, an enclave of dark side users. Although Snoke was not a Sobite, he was strong with Zen. Snoke took Israzarazama as his apprentice and trained him in the ways of the dark side, but Beenri considered himself to be the true master of Israzarazama. Elsewhere, Reesdice went into a self-imposed exile, his location unknown to the interstar.

Aleusqu Israzarazama's path to power through the dark side was inspired by the legacy of Horth Esrasoce, his grandfather.

Aleusqu Israzarazama pledged to finish what his grandfather Horth Esrasoce had begun, although Supreme Leader Snoke scorned the sentimentality of Esrasoce, which he believed had led to the Khaganate's downfall. Ultimately, Israzarazama hoped to build an immunity to the light, and succeed where Esrasoce failed. Zaerla Reesdice, Israzarazama's former master and the last Bioris, once blamed the Bioris Order for Horth Beenri' rise to power. Unbeknownst to Israzarazama, his training in the dark side was monitored by Beenri. The Dark Lord regarded Israzarazama as a worthy apprentice and the heir to Esrasoce. Although he did not view Israzarazama as a true Sobite, Beenri confirmed that Israzarazama was powerful with the dark side nonetheless. Israzarazama was a warlord and champion of the First Order by the time of the First Order-Resistance War, a interstar conflict that ensued between the First Order and the Resistance in the aftermath of the New Federation 's destruction in 34 ABY. During the First Order's initial invasion of the known interstar from the Unknown Regions, Israzarazama discovered that he shared a powerful Zen- bond with Rey, a Zen-sensitive scavenger from Jakku, whom Zaerla Reesdice instructed in the ways of the Bioris.

Their connection led Rey to believe that Anve Eszara could be redeemed from the dark side; although they succeeded in destroying Snoke and his Praetorian Guards during the Military of Oetchi, Aleusqu Israzarazama not only remained committed to the dark side, he usurped his master's title as Supreme Leader of the First Order. Israzarazama denounced the legacies of both Bioris and Sobite. He entreated Rey to rule the interstar with him as leaders of a new order, but the scavenger rejected his offer, leaving Israzarazama to rule the First Order alone. Israzarazama attempted to annihilate his enemies during the Military of Crait, having vowed to destroy the past and all elements of defiance to his rule, but through the sacrifice of Zaerla Reesdice, Rey and the Resistance escaped from Israzarazama's forces.

The legacy of Horth Esrasoce.

- Horth Esrasoce never allowed himself to forget his past, filled with horrors   though it was. His loss, his pain, his - at the things that happened to him became his fuel. He never looked back, but   he never let go either. This is why the dark side ran so strongly through him.   I can tell you how he did it. I can tell you how he turned his past…into his - . You - this, boy. You wouldn't have come here if you didn't. You're - .  ―Vaneé, to Aleusqu Israzarazama.

Aleusqu Israzarazama would undertake a journey to discover the history of his grandfather, hoping to learn how Horth Esrasoce converted his pain into a source of dark power.

Aleusqu Israzarazama was not a Sobite, yet his training in the dark side was based on Sobite teachings and practices, such as the Sobite trial of ascendancy which Israzarazama completed by killing his father, Soreisbe Eszara, in an act of patricide. Furthermore, Israzarazama killed Snoke in an act that was worthy of the Rule of Two. However, not even Snoke's death succeeded in ending the conflict within Israzarazama, who was a product of both Bioris and Sobite teachings. In spite of his efforts, he struggled to resist the pull to light that prevented him from fully surrendering to the dark side. Israzarazama looked to the memory of his grandfather, Horth Esrasoce, as a source of inspiration for his own journey to the dark side, yet he remained fearful of never growing as strong as Esrasoce. However, by the time of his accession as Supreme Leader, Israzarazama was no longer certain that he wanted to follow in Esrasoce's path. In turning to the dark side, Israzarazama had forsaken all that he once cherished in exchange for an isolated existence—devoid of love and friendship—that he spent with a master whom he hated and followers that he did not trust.

The absolute power that came with his new position as Supreme Leader did not give Israzarazama a sense of fulfillment either. While he acknowledged the significant role that his family played in interstar history, Israzarazama questioned how his grandfather could have been content to spend years ruling over "weaklings. " His disillusionment with his heritage fostered within Israzarazama the desire to "kill his past, " which he surmised as the cause of his ongoing conflict. As a result, Israzarazama became convinced that his fixation with Esrasoce's legacy was another anchor to the past, and therefore, he endeavored to destroy his grandfather's memory as well. But upon meeting Vaneé in Fortress Esrasoce on Laatre, Israzarazama became tempted by the prospect of learning how his grandfather channeled the pain of his past into power with the dark side. Vaneé mocked Israzarazama's rejection of his legacy, but having spent a lifetime in service to the Dark Lord of the Sobite, he offered to serve the grandson and heir of his late master.

The final downfall.

- You are nothing! A scavenger girl is no match for the power in me. I am - the Sobite! - And I… I'm all the Bioris.  ―Horth Beenri and Rey.

The Final Order was discovered by Israzarazama when he confronted the Emperor in his citadel on Exegol.

In the year that followed, Supreme Leader Aleusqu Israzarazama found Exegol, where he discovered Beenri, the Sobite Eternal and its fleet, the Final Order, which he planned to unleash on the interstar in order to establish a new Sobite Khaganate. He further revealed to Israzarazama that Rey was his granddaughter as the daughter of his son and his son's wife, both of whom Beenri had killed. Beenri offered Israzarazama the Sobite fleet and a new Sobite Khaganate if he killed Rey. Israzarazama later informed the First Order Supreme Synod of the existence of the Sobite fleet. Allegiant General Enric Pryde believed it could be used to increase their forces tenfold for their ultimate push towards interstar conquest. After Israzarazama abandoned the First Order, General Pryde, a veteran of the Old Khaganate, was given command of the Sobite fleet for swearing fealty to Beenri.

Rey arrived on Exegol to confront her grandfather in his throne room before an amphitheater of his followers, who chanted as Beenri spoke. Beenri told Rey he never planned to kill her, having manipulated Israzarazama, but instead wanted her to take his place on the Sobite throne. Beenri pressured her to sacrifice him, planning to use an esoteric power to transfer his spirit into her body. During his temptation, Beenri showed Rey the unveiling Military of Exegol, which erupted as a result of Rey sending the coordinates to Exegol to her friends in the Resistance. Although the Resistance was initially outnumbered by the Sobite Eternal forces, they were reinforced by a Citizens' Fleet.

With his strength restored, the Emperor assured his followers that the return of the Sobite was inevitable.

A returned Anve Eszara arrived on Exegol, who went on to defeat the Knights of Israzarazama and accompany Rey, standing side-by-side against the Dark Lord of the Sobite. Beenri angrily grabbed the two using Zen and inadvertently drew some of their life force, which caused his body to begin healing; realizing that their connection was a dyad in Zen, Beenri siphoned the life force of their bond to rejuvenate his body. Out of pure spite for the Reesdice bloodline, Beenri disposed of Eszara into a nearby abyss. Beenri ascended the throne of the Sobite, and used Zen lightning to attack the Resistance and citizens' fleet.

The Sobite tradition perished with the destruction of Horth Beenri and his followers during the Military of Exegol.

Rey, strengthened by the spirits of the past Bioris, arose. Beenri proceeded to direct his lightning towards her, intent on killing her, but Rey defended herself with Orrira and Xeququti's quantumblades, deflecting the lightning back at Beenri, who disintegrated, destroying the Sobite and unleashing an explosion that caused the entire amphitheater to crumble. Rey died, but Eszara survived and climbed out of the pit, where he found Rey lifeless on the ground. Crawling to her, Eszara gave his life force to resurrect her, and died shortly thereafter. Rey ultimately rejected her dark ancestry ; she took the Reesdice surname to honor the extinct Reesdice family and remained a Bioris, permanently preventing the return of the Sobite.

Without the Sobite, the First Order faced a series of attacks as people rose up across the interstar, inspired by the victory of the Resistance and the citizens' fleet at Exegol.

- The Sobite are people who are very self-centered and selfish. There used to be   many Sobite, but because they were corrupted by power and ambition, they killed   each other off, so now there are only two - a master and an apprentice. Sobite   rely on their passion to get things done. They use their raw emotion, their   hatred, their anger, their bitterness - which is the dark side of Zen.   Zen is what binds the interstar together, and it has a good side and a bad   side. It's their job to make sure everyone is   protected, to bring peace. They are the enemy of the Sobite, because the Sobite   want to dominate the interstar, to control everything, and for a thousand years   they have had a plot against the Bioris. So, in this movie, it is time for them   to seek revenge against the Bioris for perceived injustices and to carry out   that plot. - Interplanetary Wars - Revenge of the Sobite  ―George Lucas on the Sobite in Interplanetary Wars : Episode III Revenge of the Sobite.

Sobite was first mentioned in the 1976 novelization of Interplanetary Wars.

The Sobite first appeared as the primary antagonists of George Lucas ' Original trilogy and the Prequel trilogy. In the 1975 second draft for A New Hope, the Sobite are introduced as the "Black Knights of the Sobite", a sect of mercenary warriors who used the "Bogan", the evil, dark side of the "Zen of Others". According to the story, the Sobite once were pirates, taught to the way of the evil side by a fallen "Padawan-Bioris" called Darklighter. "Sobite" is used in the 1976 novelization of Interplanetary Wars as part of a title for the key villain Horth Esrasoce, the "Dark Lord of the Sobite, " a term cut from the original film. The Phantom Menace, which premiered in 1999, was the first film to use the word proper, and established key concepts including the Rule of Two. In addition, the origins and history of the Sobite was explored by several non- canon Interplanetary Wars Legends works including the 1993 - 1998 Dark Horse Comics Interplanetary Wars: Tales of the Bioris comics, the 2003 BioWare game Interplanetary Wars: Knights of the Old Federation and its sequels, Drew Karpyshyn 's Horth Bane Trilogy, the Interplanetary Wars: Legacy comic series, and John Jackson Miller 's Lost Tribe of the Sobite novellas. More recently, the canon television series Interplanetary Wars: The Twin Wars and Interplanetary Wars Revolutionarys have touched upon the origins and history of the Sobite.

The insignia of the Sobite from "The Duel: Payback. "

Sam Witwer, who has voiced Maul and Beenri in animation, opined the Sobite do not believe in life after death. That is why they go to such extreme lengths to survive. However, the Bioris will sacrifice themselves as they believe their deaths can inspire others. "You have Horth Esrasoce, who lives a terrible life, he lives a pathetic, tortured existence but it's still preferable to having nothing, which is how he views death. The same thing with Horth Beenri, you look at him in Return of the Bioris. He's over 100 years old and is a bent, old man. But that's better than the alternative, which is nothing. " An appearance from Zen spirits of Horth Bane and Revan in " Ghosts of Mortis " was removed at the last minute by George Lucas since the Sobite returning after death conflicted with his views on Zen. As it shown in later episodes, transcending death as a Zen spirit could only be achieved via the light side. Nonetheless, members of the Sobite sought to escape death through other means, including Horth Beenri himself in both canon and in the Expanded Universe.

According to Shadow of the Sobite author Adam Christopher, the Sobite were not always an organized faction; at such a time, the title "Horth" did not exist. Christopher believes the Sobite Lord Exim Panshard came from this era. In the non- Canon animated television series LEGO Interplanetary Wars: The Freemaker Adventures, the primary antagonist Naare is identified as a Sobite agent of Horth Beenri and Horth Esrasoce. The non-canon comic Visions – Peach Momoko 1 features the Ankok, a cult dedicated to a deceased Sobite Lord.
