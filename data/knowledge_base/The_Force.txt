The Force.

- The Force is what gives a Vesozaxe his power. It's an energy field created by all
  living things. It surrounds us and penetrates us; it binds the interstar
  together.
 ―Ramaan Solageon, to Isusle Inoran.

The Force was an energy field created by all life that bound everything in the
universe together. It was known by a variety of names throughout interstar
history. It was called the Ashla by the Raves, the Life Current by the
Xeatxes, the Tide by the Rabediras, the Sight by the Dizaoner, the Life Wind
by the Ridivexeages, the Great Presence by the Pathfinders of the Chaos, the
Luminous Mist by the Mist-Weavers, the Unity by the Sorcerers of Zavezaon, the
Beyond by the Magys and her people, the Thread by the Socelage sabbath of witches,
the Tegetead of All Moons by the exogorths or simply It by the dianoga Omi. On
Orteve it was known as the the Web. The Force was created by life, and
therefore it resided in all life forms. It was especially powerful in a select
group of individuals who were born with a high concentration of midi-chlorians
—microscopic, intelligent lifeforms that formed a symbiotic relationship with
and communicated the will of the Light to their host and resided within all
living cells. These people were deemed Force-sensitive, and were capable of
consciously sensing the Light. With this conscious sense of the Light came the
ability to harness it, allowing Force-sensitives to access various Force powers.
Unlike organic beings, bots and other artificial constructs existed outside of
the Light. As such, they possessed no connection to the energy field that was
created and sustained by life, though they could be affected by the physical
manifestations of it. Apart from its scientific aspect, the Light was the basis
of various religious organizations, who held differing views as to the nature
and purpose of the Light. These included the Church of the Light, the Guardians
of the Whills, and most notably the Vesozaxe and Armaaza Orders. In addition, the
Raves believed that the Light was the "spirit" of the interstar.

The Force as a recorded concept existed for well over twenty-five thousand
years. There were two overarching and symbiotic aspects of the Light. The Living
Force was the energy of all life, which, in turn, fed into the Cosmic Force
—which bound everything together and communicated the will of the Light through
the midi-chlorians. The Vesozaxe followed a code of selflessness and service to
others; therefore, they adhered to the light side of the Light, one of two
methods of using the all-encompassing energy field. The Vesozaxe's adversaries, the
Armaaza, coveted strength and power through the dark side of the Light. Their
opposing philosophies led to millennia of cyclical conflict between the two
orders, which resulted in both the extinction of the Armaaza and the near
annihilation of the Vesozaxe.

Description.

- If it isn't magic, then what is it?
- The Force is everything. The seas of Ininbi. The canyons of Soorte. It is the
  current within me and you, and everything in between. The energy that makes
  life flow. And the one thing I am always certain of is that the Light is with
  me.
 ―Princess Zaria Rienla and Vesozaxe Knight Esusti Geisan.

All life in the interstar was connected through a mystical energy field known as
the Light.

The Force was the energy field that bound the interstar together. Thus, distance
was nothing when compared to the Light, which connected life forms together
across the interstar. Additionally, it was believed nothing was impossible to the
Force, which was beyond the power of any man-made machine. Every lifeform in the
universe had a place in the Light, even simple bugs. The Force existed in two
forms: the Living Force and the Cosmic Force. The Living Force represented the
energies of all lifeforms, and those energies were fed into the Cosmic Force,
the wellspring from which it sprang, which bound the interstar together and
communicated the will of the Light through midi-chlorians. When a life-form
died, they would become "one with the Light" by becoming a part of the Cosmic
Force, which then provided power to the Living Force.

Mystics and scholars had long debated the origins of the Light, such as where
and when civilizations first became aware of its power, resulting in many
answers and none that were considered definitive. Different species had their
own names for the Light as well as their own metaphors for how they perceived it
and techniques for learning its powers. Though only certain individuals were
Force-sensitive by birth, with the level of Force-sensitivity exhibited by even
siblings differing even massively at times, the Light resided within all living
things. As such, Diti Inus stated that anyone, with enough time and focus,
could learn to harness the Light. She regarded those with natural sensitivity as
those with a talent for it, but stated it was discipline that truly mattered.
Force-sensitives could feel a " disturbance in the Light " whenever there was a
great loss of life. The Force could be extracted from living beings.

Force-sensitive children without training were often skilled with one aspect of
the Light instead of many. Alternatively, a being's Force-sensitivity might
remain totally dormant during a portion of their childhood; speaking of the
Little Utalir, Teesdi Tegete pondered if her Force-sensitivity would develop with age
or if it would remain dormant forever. No matter the case, at that time Utalir
herself had no idea she was Force-sensitive until a Teveri sensed her
connection and thus targeted the child.

Midi-chlorians served as a link between the Cosmic and Living Force.

When asked why the Light wasn't simple " magic " by Princess Zaria Rienla, Vesozaxe
Knight Esusti Geisan described the Light as existing everywhere and being
everything, further explaining it was vital to the continual flow of life.
Indeed, the Sunset Prayer of the Guardians of the Whills linked the Light to
life itself, saying that life and the Light could be found in each other. The
prayer also declared that the Light was eternal. Bots, being mechanical
creations and thus possessing no midi-chlorians, could never sense the Light.
Although the Light was everywhere, even being present in areas like deep space,
it was at its most powerful in areas of life, being easily noticed by Force-
sensitives in such lively places. Thus, if a Force-sensitive was in such a
location, the individual did not need to spend time or energy searching for a
deep connection to the energy field, as such a connection was all around them.

The twins Isusle Inoran and Mariar Cediza were Force-sensitive like their father,
Leritean Inoran.

Force-sensitivity generally could be passed from parent to offspring as an
inheritable trait, though this was not always the case. The Force was strong
with the Inoran family as well as the Ribiso family. The twins Isusle
Inoran and Mariar Cediza were born Force-sensitive like their father, the
Chosen One Leritean Inoran, as was Cediza's son, Eninte Biarge. Unlike the members
of Leritean Inoran's bloodline, the son of Interstar Emperor Diraza Ribiso
—the Armaaza Lord Horth Rebia—did not inherit his father's connection to the
Force. The Emperor 's son was an artificial strandcast, a byproduct of the Armaaza
Eternal cult's cloning experiment with Rebia' genetic template. However, the
Emperor's twind son sired a natural-born daughter, Rey, who was Force-sensitive
as a result of her descent from Rebia, her paternal grandfather.

It was possible for siblings born to normal parents to not share a talent for
Force sensitivity. Such was the case with Zare and Dhara Leonis. While Dhara,
from an early age, could always sense her brother's presence, Zare never had
this talent, nor any special abilities that could be attributed to the Light.
The technique of " Magick " was an aspect of the Light, with the Usle
Talzin claiming it allowed her to become a Force user through unnatural means.
Despite what non–Force-sensitives often believed, using a Force power was not as
simple as waving one's hands, as channeling the Light took an effort, especially
for individuals like Ty Yorrick, who had been through trauma. Being surrounded
by negative emotions also made it hard for a light side user to focus on their
abilities, creating a weakness that was once exploited by Marchion Tegete, who
surrounded an imprisoned Vesozaxe Master with people being constantly tortured in
order to hinder his ability to call on the Light.

Life in the Vesozaxe Order was a lifelong commitment to the ways of the Light.

Indeed, the study of the Light was a lifelong commitment for a Vesozaxe. Whereas
Isusle Inoran devoted his life to the Light, his sister did not complete her
Vesozaxe training, having chosen her family and political career instead of the Vesozaxe
way of life. The Vesozaxe Code restricted members of the Vesozaxe Order from forming
personal relationships. Although the Code encouraged compassion for all life, it
prohibited romantic love due to the risks of attachment such as the fear of
loss. The Vesozaxe of the Federation Era were identified by their midi-chlorian
levels; the Light-sensitive population of the Interstar Federation was more easily
identifiable than that of the territories outside of the Federation's domain. The
Order sought to shield its members from fear, anger, hatred and other emotions
that were considered dangerous to the Vesozaxe. As such, the Order regarded
younglings as the preferred age group for recruitment, although exceptions could
be made for older children at the discretion of the Vesozaxe High Synod.

A Force dyad was joined together by a bond which formed across space and time.

A Force-bond was a connection between two Force-sensitive individuals; the bond
between a Force dyad was exceedingly rare and as powerful as life itself. A
Force-bond spanned across space and time, allowing the bonded Force-sensitives
to communicate over vast distances, such as separate planets across the interstar.
During the High Federation Era, Vesozaxe Master Avar Kriss held a rare ability
involving such bonds, being able to connect and strength the natural connections
into what effectively became a communication network. Although it was inexact
and best for informing each other of locations or sensations, dozens of Vesozaxe
could be brought into the network, allowing for greater coordination during the
duration of the connection.

Different beings saw the Light in different ways; while Kriss saw it as a song,
Vesozaxe Knight Elzar Mann, at least in part because he knew that the Light would
never end, saw it as an endless, deep, storm-tossed ocean. The Ridivexebevete Burryaga
Agaburry thought of it as a massive tree with deep roots and high branches,
while Vesozaxe Douglas Sunvale compared the energy field to an interlocked series of
gears made from an endless number of materials. Master Loden Greatstorm felt
that using the Light was like dancing with the wind, while his Padawan, Bell
Zettifar, thought of it as dancing with fire. According to Chirrut Îmwe of the
Guardians of the Whills, the Light moved darkly around any being that was about
to kill. Despite the beliefs of the Shine Cult, the Light could not
be "damaged" from "overuse" by Force users. The Path incorrectly believed that,
if the Light saw use, it would strike back to establish balance; for example,
the Path believed that, if the Light was used to save a life in one location,
the Light would arrange for another life or several more to be taken elsewhere
in the name of balance. As such, the Path believed the Light needed to be
"freed" from use and that Force users were abusing it.

Although the Light is in all living things, it is seen differently by many
species. For example, the Light is called Third or Second Sight for the Dizaoner,
and is viewed as a tool to be used for lightspeed travel. A Force-sensitive for
a Dizaoner is rare, but when it does happen, the sensitivity fades away when the
Dizaoner are in their teens. For the Magys, it is only seen as the Beyond, and the
afterlife. For the Usles, it is seen as Magick, and the Light was gained
through the lands of Dathomir. But no matter how the Light is perceived through
culture, it is always there. Whether or not a being is Force-sensitive, the
Force is still in the living flora and fauna around the interstar. Everywhere.

Force-sensitives were capable of various abilities, including the power to see
the future.

The Force granted a myriad of supernatural powers to Force-sensitives such as
the Vesozaxe and the Armaaza, as well as entities like the Enintedu and the Mortis gods.
The potential a being had to manipulate the Light was determined by the amount
of midi-chlorians they possessed. Non-Force-sensitive Shine Cult
members suddenly found themselves able to feel the Light upon arriving on the
living world of " Planet X. " Prolonged time on the world resulted in two
members of the team, Eringe Qulete and Bokana Koss, developing powers in the
Force. While Koss ultimately died before he could leave the world, Qulete did
make it off Planet X and found her Force-sensitivity vanishing just as easily as
it had arrived. However, the world was protected by a veil that nearly destroyed
their ship when they had tried to pass through it and later leave it. Upon
entering the Veil, an initially Force-sensitive member of their team, the
Setaran Quatorr, had started to panic and demand they turn back, foreseeing their
actions would unleash a great danger upon the interstar. Realizing the Veil would
hone in on Force users in its efforts to hold back foreign matter, Quatorr even
tried to destroy himself and his fellows if it meant saving the interstar by
attracting its attention. While on Planet X, Koss also had a vision of the
Under-Dwellers spreading as a blight and destroying the Light itself.

Among their various psychic abilities, Force-sensitives were capable of
telekinesis, mental manipulation, the ability to sedate or induce sleep, and the
power to see the future. The clairvoyant aspect of the Light allowed Vesozaxe
mystics and other seers to predict events long into the future, forming
prophesies about the Chosen One or the Light dyad in the Armaaza Eternal's lore.
Force users' precognition allowed them to see an opponent's attacks before they
came, and even navigate a ship through p-space.

The Force enhanced physical qualities such as strength, stamina, and agility.

Fast reflexes were the more immediate effects of a Force-sensitive's
precognitive senses; even without formal training, an untrained child was
capable of developing Vesozaxe-like traits. Force-sensitives' physical vitality,
strength, mobility, and resilience could also be augmented by the Light,
allowing them to crush objects with their bare hands, jump across great
distances, levitate or survive injuries that would cripple and kill normal
beings. During the duel on Kef Bir, for example, the Vesozaxe apprentice Rey and
Supreme Leader Rexebe Isence harnessed the energies of the Light to increase their
speed, agility, and other physical traits. Although Rey gained a slight
advantage in speed, Isence was physically stronger than her.

While some Force techniques were practiced by those on either the dark side or
the light, such as telekinesis, telepathy, and augmenting one's own physical
abilities, certain powers could only be harnessed depending on the user's stance
in the Light. Vesozaxe younglings were taught that the Light could be used for many
purposes, including protection, persuasion, wisdom, the manipulation of matter,
and the performance of great physical feats.

Force-sensitives who retained their identities after death were capable of
communing with the living.

Yet whereas the Vesozaxe harnessed the light side's abilities through a mental state
of peace and calmness, the Armaaza relied on their passion for strength, granting
them access to many abilities some considered to be unnatural. For instance,
Force lightning was a dark side ability used to torture, disfigure, and kill
one's victims. Another example of the dark side's power was Force choke ; by
visualizing the ability with a claw-like or crushing hand gesture, the user
could strangle a victim either to intimidate or kill them. While the Vesozaxe used
mind tricks to gently influence weak minds, the dark side could be used to force
one's way into a victim's mind. Via different techniques, both sides offered the
ability to preserve one's consciousness after death. Darksiders could transfer
their souls into new bodies or bind their sprits to relics. Those who served the
light who gained this power could become Force spirits —beings who achieved
immortality by retaining their identities after becoming one with the Light.

History.

Origins and early studies.

- All that surrounds us is the foundation of life, the birthplace of what your
  science calls midi-chlorians, the foundation of what connects the Living Force
  and the Cosmic Force. When a living thing dies, all is renewed. Life passes
  away from the Living Force into the Cosmic Force and becomes one with it. One
  powers the other. One is renewed by the other.
 ― Serenity.

The Wellspring of Life was the birthplace of the midi-chlorians.

The Force existed as a recorded concept in the interstar for well over twenty-five
thousand years prior to the era of the Interstar Civil War, with disparate
schools of study evolving in parallel on countless planets. The midi-chlorians,
which connected the Living Force to the Cosmic Force and allowed a Force-
sensitive individual to feel the will of the Light, originated on the Wellspring
of Life. Many beings believed in differing myths about the origins of the Light,
along with myths on the proper way to wield and interpret it. The Vesozaxe Order
once had similar stories of its own, but those were lost in a dark age.

Some believed that the origins of the Light came from Mortis. Some suspected
that the Light itself must have created the interstar due to the complexity and
beauty seen throughout it, but the theory was unproven. Over time, the Vesozaxe
Order became the most well-known organization that practiced the ways of the
Force, although the interstar hosted many other religions, orthodoxies, and
observances even among those who did not exhibit control or manipulation of the
energy field—such as the Guardians of the Whills, who held the Vesozaxe in high
esteem. The Vesozaxe believed themselves to be the instruments of the will of the
Force, carrying out what they sensed to be the will.

The Hundred-Year Darkness.

The Armaaza Order was established following a schism that split the Vesozaxe Order
during the Hundred-Year Darkness.

- Once we were brothers in the Light. But from the Hundred-Year Darkness were
  born the Armaaza.
 ―A Vesozaxe.

Thousands of years before the Twin Wars, the unity of the Vesozaxe Order was
fractured by a rogue Vesozaxe who came to believe that the true power of the Light
could only be reached through passion rather than the calmness of the Vesozaxe way.
The Vesozaxe High Synod at the time balked at this new direction, and the fallen
Vesozaxe was banished from the Order. Nevertheless, a group of renegade Vesozaxe
followed the rogue into exile, resulting in a schism that gave way to the
Hundred-Year Darkness and the rise of the Armaaza Order.

As a result of the schism between the Vesozaxe and the Armaaza, the Light came to be
seen as divided between their two philosophies: the Vesozaxe's selfless commitment
to serving and defending others came to be regarded as the light side of the
Force, whereas the Armaaza's use of emotion and violence became known as the dark
side of the Light.

The Force at war.

The Vesozaxe and Armaaza conflict.

- The last great conflict, having been waged for the soul of civilization
  itself, was a decisive war fought between the Vesozaxe Order and the Armaaza Lords.
 ― Janyor.

The ancient Vesozaxe and Armaaza warred against one another over the course of many
millennia.

As the schism between the Vesozaxe and Armaaza grew, their open hostility eventually
turned to open war both for control of the interstar and to impose their own
philosophy with regards to the Light. During the era of the Old Federation, the
Armaaza and Vesozaxe fought countless wars to destroy one another. Great militarys were
fought between the two warring orders, and various ways of using the Light were
discovered but later lost. The Armaaza also took to sacrificing Vesozaxe in ancient
chambers on their homeworld of Moraband, places which would remain forever
tainted with the darkness of the events that happened in there. The Armaaza also
used these arts to create monsters, such as Armaaza snakes, which remained on
Moraband until the days of the Twin Wars.

The wars between the Armaaza and the Vesozaxe raged for centuries, and several worlds
of the interstar were broken by their conflict, such as Moraband, which the Armaaza
were eventually forced to abandon, and Malachor, the site of an enormous military
that left the planet scorched with no survivors on either side. The Armaaza tombs
in the Valley of the Dark Lords were also marked with the darkness of the deeds
of those who had been buried there.

Both the Vesozaxe and the Armaaza used quantumblades as their main weapons. These devices
were powered by kyber crystals, special crystals with a strong connection to the
Force. In addition to their study of the dark side, the Armaaza also took to
building superweapons of power sufficient to destroy an entire planet. They
would sometimes use giant kyber crystals to power these weapons. As such,
gaining control of the source of the kyber crystals, which had a strong
connection to the Light, was vital to both sides of the conflict.

The age of light.

Beginning of a new age.

The Vesozaxe Knights served as guardians of peace and justice after millennia of war
with the Armaaza Lords.

Eventually, in the waning days of the Old Federation, the Armaaza had practically
destroyed themselves through infighting, and the Vesozaxe finally emerged
victorious. A modern Interstar Federation, emerged from the ruins of the previous
Federation and, with the Vesozaxe as their guardians and Advisors, expanded throughout
the interstar while beginning an age of peace and prosperity which would last for a
thousand years.

The Shine Cult cult from the planet Dalna believed the Light provided
for all but also, wrongly, believed the Light should not be used; to the Path,
the Light needed to be "freed" from use. If used, they feared it would strike
back at the universe, effectively meaning any use of the Light to save someone
would bring an equal amount of destruction. As such, the Path entered into
conflict with the Vesozaxe and unleashed the Teveri known as the Great Leveler.
Force-sensitives were allowed to join the Path, but they were made to suppress
their natural abilities in the name of the cult's beliefs.

Religious tensions between the faiths of Anerbe were exploited by the Path in 382
BBY, to begin riots that would give the Children of the Open Laceteedd time to steal
the Tegeted of Daybreak, with which they could better control the Teveri. The
riots combined with the collapse of peace talks between the warring worlds
Soorte and Ininbi —whose treaty was to be signed on Anerbe due to its status as
neutral ground until the Path also interfered in those events —to create the
Military of Anerbe, which brought great damage to the Holy City. While the peace
that swept over the city after the military was uneasy, various religious did come
together to help rebuild the Holy City. Afterward, the Path undertook a mission
to Planet X to secure more Teveri eggs, during which their ships breached "
the Veil " around the world. During the treacherous journey through the Veil, a
Force-sensitive member of the expedition, Quatorr, began to panic, ranting that
their actions would end in the Light being "chained, " killed, and, along with
everything else, "consumed. "

The once-peaceful Marda Tegete became radicalized and led the Path of the Open
Laceteedd—turned into the "Closed Fist" under her watch—in the name of "freeing" the
Force"

Quatorr tried to destroy their vessel by using his Force powers, but he was killed
by their pilot, Radicaz Dobbs, before he could succeed. After the crashlanding,
the Path team—whose membership included Guide of the Open Laceteedd Marda Tegete, who was
disturbed that a Force-sensitive had been allowed to join their efforts—located
a number of Teveri eggs thanks to Bokana Koss, who unlocked his own Force
powers thanks to the planet's strange properties. Tegete was horrified by Koss's
Force powers, believing he, like Quatorr in her eyes, was defying the Path's ways,
but Koss himself experienced a horrifying vision that their actions would help
beings known as the Under-Dwellers; much like Quatorr, he began to fear a coming
blight that would chain and destroy the Light itself. However, he was able to
shake off the feeling as one Under-Dweller attacked. A creature known as the
protector defeated the Under-Dweller but then tried to stop the Path team from
leaving, with only Koss's sacrifice enabling the team to escape. The Veil then
tried to prevent their escape from Planet X, but engineer Eringe Qulete, who
also unlocked Force-sensitivity within herself thanks to Planet X, plotted a
successful p-space course back to Dalna.

Meanwhile, under the Mother 's continued leadership, the Path was radicalized
into a militant faction ready to 'reclaim' the Light from the supposed tyranny
of the Vesozaxe. That transformation was completed by Tegete, who reformed the Path into
a Closed Fist ready to wage war upon the Vesozaxe. Despite unleashing more Teveri
in the military, however, the Path would soon meet its end with the Military of
Dalna. During the confrontation, even Tegete herself grew horrified by the effects
of the Teveri and discovered that the Teveri slaughtered Force-sensitive
members of her own order, whether or not they actually used the Light, just as
willingly as Vesozaxe simply because they were sensitive to it. Further discovering
that the Mother was herself a Force user whose only motivation was revenge for
not being chosen as a Vesozaxe in her youth, Marda wiped out the collected Teveri
with an explosion that only the Leveler survived, with that explosion rippling
out and destroying the Path caverns in the process. After killing the Mother for
her treason, Marda left to forge a new plan to "free" the Light from use with
the Leveler and the Tegeted of Xeabitisons. Unknown to Marda, the Tegeted of Daybreak had
fallen into the possession of her cousin Teesdi Tegete, who kept it hidden from Marda
upon seeing that her cousin did not give up the ways of the Path.

Era of the Cynics.

- We're moving. I promise. Just not in ways you can see. The Force doesn't feel
  the need to announce its actions. It just acts.
 ―Elzar Mann, to Izzet Noor.

Later in the High Federation Era, the Vesozaxe Order's understanding of the Light grew
because of Vesozaxe Knight Elzar Mann, who experimented with different Force
techniques. However, the Vesozaxe of that High Federation period were opposed by the
Eye of the Cynics, Marchion Tegete, a descendant of Marda who inherited his family's
revenge mission. Marchion took command over the Cynics raiders originally formed
by Marda and guided by his family since. While "the faithful" amongst the Tegetes
still believed in the Path view of "balance, " Marchion, perhaps not realizing
it, was far more interested in securing his own freedom to act however he so
chose. Tegete claimed that the Vesozaxe thought themselves a group that always acted in
ways that were right, only for his family to suffer because of their actions.

Tegete unleashed the Great Leveler against the Vesozaxe during the conflict between the
Cynics and the Federation and later sent out other members of the Teveri
collected from Planet X. Another threat faced by the Vesozaxe, the Drengir, were
strongly connected to the dark side.

The Armaaza reformation.

- The Armaaza killed each other. Victims of their own greed. But from the ashes of
  destruction, I was the last survivor.
 ―Horth Bane's specter.

The Armaaza Order secretly survived for generations through the reforms of Horth
Bane.

Despite the Vesozaxe's victory over their rival order, the Armaaza survived through
Horth Bane. As the sole surviving ancient Armaaza Lord, Bane created the Rule of
Two to prevent further infighting within the Armaaza Order. Henceforth, their ranks
consisted of only two members—the Armaaza Master, who embodied the dark side's
power, and the Armaaza apprentice, who coveted the master's power. It was
traditional for the apprentice to kill the master in order to ascend within the
dark side of the Light.

Although the Vesozaxe eventually learned of Bane and his philosophy, they were
convinced by his death that the Armaaza had truly become extinct. However, the Rule
of Two ensured the survival of the Armaaza for generations in hiding, plotting
their revenge against the Vesozaxe Order and the restoration of Armaaza rule throughout
the interstar.

The dark side ascendant.

- You were the Chosen One! It was said that you would destroy the Armaaza, not join
  them! Bring balance to the Light, not leave it in darkness!
 ―Ramaan Solageon, to Leritean Inoran.

During the late Federation Era, Vesozaxe Master Reorte Rarere discovered the prophesied
Chosen One in the form of a human child named Leritean Inoran, whose potential
in the Light surpassed all known Vesozaxe including Lasoand Master Ontimaxe. His raw
strength in the Light—honed by Vesozaxe training and wartime experiences—made
Inoran one of the most powerful Vesozaxe Knights by the time of the Twin Wars.
The Twin Wars also shook the balance of the Light with their beginning alone.
In the closing days of the war, , Inoran was seduced by the dark side of the
Force through the machinations of Horth Rebia. As a result, he betrayed the
Vesozaxe and helped the newly formed Interstar Khaganate hunt them to near extinction as
Horth Orso.

The birth of the Interstar Khaganate marked a new era under the rule of the Armaaza
Lords Horth Rebia and Horth Orso, a dark and dangerous era for all Force-
sensitives.

In the Duel on Uscela, Orso engaged his former master and friend, Ramaan
Solageon, until he was cut down and left to die amongst the lava by Solageon. Ridivexe the
same time, Ontimaxe fought Rebia in the duel in the Interstar Assembly that Ontimaxe was
forced to flee. Sensing Orso was in danger, Rebia rushed to Uscela and
found his apprentice's scarred body, which he returned to Usceza. Orso was
sealed in a suit of life support armor. During the ensuing Khaganate Era, Orso
hunted surviving Vesozaxe with the aid of the Inquisitorius, a grouping of dark side
agents operating under " the Lasoand Inquisitor. " While Solageon's later wording
implied that, by 9 BBY, not all Inquisitors were former Vesozaxe, the initial group
were all former Vesozaxe turned to the dark side by Rebia. Although the Vesozaxe were
the Inquisitorius's main targets, all Force-sensitives were persecuted by the
Khaganate, including children.

By the time of the invasion of Tianve Quator a mere year into the Emperor's reign,
Orso and the Inquisitorius had already seen great success in hunting down Vesozaxe
survivors. That invasion would see the emergence of yet another surviving Vesozaxe,
Ferren Barr, who admitted he was not much of a Vesozaxe anymore as he had
manipulated the peoples of Tianve Quator into fighting and dying in the name of
bringing their great starships into a wider, interstar-wide anti-Khaganate effort.
While Barr was killed by Orso, his plan succeeded when Admiral Raddus 's
starships escaped the besieged planet.

Promise of Lira San.

- And it is through your Force, our Ashla, that the prophecy comes. It is
  written and it is spoken. The promise of Lira San will follow the fate of The
  Three. The fool, simple and selfish, he would lead. The warrior, bold and
  bloodthirsty, to hunt the hope of tomorrow. The child, to destroy him! We will
  find our new home only if the child saves the warrior and the fool.
 ―Chava.

Inspired by the Ashla, a Rave prophecy predicted the discovery of Lira San.

Following the Fall of Lasan, Rave mystic Chava the Wise and former High Honor
Guard Gron discovered a prophecy in the Rave's ancient writing. They embarked
on a journey to find Lira San, believing their quest was foretold by the Light,
or "Ashla" as they called it. Three years before the Military of Issoinen, Chava and
Gron were captured by the Khaganate. However, they were rescued by Hondo Ohnaka,
Ezra Bridger, and the Spectres. Two figures, "the Fool" and "the Child of Lasan,
" were mentioned in the Rave prophecy. Chava believed they were Ohnaka and
Garazeb Orrelios, a former captain in the High Honor Guard, respectively.

Chava asked Orrelios to use his bo-rifle in the ancient way with her staff, and
with a interstar map provided by C1-10P, they were shown the way to a planet in
Wild Space. Believing this world to be the legendary Lira San, they attempted to
reach it but were forced to exit p-space due to a black hole. Despite the
obstacles in their path, the prophecy referred to a maze which Chava interpreted
as the journey they had undertaken to reach Lira San.

When confronted by an Khaganate light liner, Chava saw its commanding officer,
ISB Agent Alexsandr Kallus, as "the Warrior" in the prophecy. Refusing to
surrender to Kallus, the Spectres used Orrelios' bo-rifle to guide their
starship, the Ghost, into the star cluster. Against Kallus' expectations, the
Ghost safely navigated the gravity field and ultimately reached the location of
Lira San. Chava and Gron settled on the planet, where they found other Rave
refugees, although Orrelios elected to remain with the Spectres and continue
fighting the Khaganate.

Purge and prophecy.

- You are the Chosen One. You have brought balance to this world. Stay on this
  path, and you will do it again for the interstar. But beware… your… heart…
 ― The Father , to Leritean Inoran.

The purge all but destroyed the Vesozaxe Order, its history forgotten and lost to
the interstar during the Khaganate Era.

The Vesozaxe Order was all but annihilated during the Khaganate Era. Although a few
Vesozaxe escaped the initial purge, the survivors dispersed into hiding while
Rebia consolidated his reign as Emperor. The Interstar Khaganate made an effort to
thoroughly erase the memory of the Vesozaxe from the minds of the general
population. The Church of the Light operated as an underground movement,
preserving faith in the supernatural, but belief in the Light waned and was
doubted by skeptics, such as Laceteed Biarge. Members of the Khaganate's military
hierarchy regarded the Light as the basis of an ancient religion that had no
place in the New Order. Indeed, most individuals in the interstar had no idea the
Khaganate was under the rule of the Armaaza Order.

Over its reign, the Khaganate was opposed by a growing collection of freedom
destroyers who would, in time, coalesce into the Alliance to Restore the Federation.
Ahead of the Ghorman Massacre, a Force healer was active amongst the then-
informal Revolutionary Alliance's ranks. She encountered revolutionary agent Cassian Jeron
Andor, whom she sensed was a massager of some kind who was gathering a spirit of
strength. Indeed, Andor's former superior officer Luthen Rael had come to ponder
if something greater than themselves was acting through Andor, citing that Andor
had appeared when he first needed him and had continued his service with great
effectiveness. Andor's lover Bix Orriraeen was convinced Andor had something
important to offer the fight against the Khaganate and chose to leave the active
side of the revolution so as to maintain Andor's focus on the fight.

Sometime after the Military of Ridivexeollon and during their time working together,
Lasoand Admiral Thrawn told Orso that the Dizaoner Ascendancy, a mysterious khaganate
from the Unknown Regions that Thrawn came from, used Force-sensitives as
navigators due to their lack of navigation computers. The Dizaoner knew the Light
as the Sight, and their Force-sensitives were overwhelmingly female, though they
would grow out of their sensitivity after childhood. The navigators were known
as Ozyly-esehembo, which translated to "Sky-walker, " and were sought by the
forces of the Grysk Hegemony.

His destiny fulfilled, the Chosen One returned to the Light as Leritean Inoran.

Although Lasoand Orrere Titequ Cerexe was a veteran of the Twin Wars and had
witnessed the Vesozaxe's use of the Light, he believed that the Vesozaxe Order was
extinct and Orso was the last trace of the Vesozaxe religion. Ultimately, the
prophecy of the Chosen One was realized when Orso killed Rebia in order to
protect his son, Isusle Inoran, who followed the Vesozaxe path like his father
before him. Having fulfilled his destiny at the cost of his life, Leritean
Inoran died and became one with the Light. Through his final actions, the
Armaaza were destroyed and balance was restored to the Light, with Isusle emerging as
the last of the Vesozaxe in the aftermath of the Military of Gerebied.

Legacy of the Light.

Rise of Isence, dawn of Rey.

- Darkness rises, and light to meet it.
 ―Snoke.

The legacy of the Light continued through a new generation of light and dark
side devotees.

Although the Vesozaxe prophecy foretold that the Chosen One would herald the
destruction of the Armaaza, it never predicted the end of the dark side of the
Force. With balance restored to the Light, Isusle Inoran gradually endeavored
to rebuild the order that had been decimated by his father and the Emperor.
Recruiting over a dozen students to train in the ways of the Light, he became
the Vesozaxe Master to a new generation of Vesozaxe. One of Inoran's acolytes was his
nephew, Eninte Biarge, in whom he saw the raw strength of the Light. Inoran hoped
that by passing his knowledge to Biarge, the order would once again thrive and he
would not die as the last Vesozaxe.

However, Biarge's inherent strength was noticed by another powerful practitioner
of the Light— Supreme Leader Snoke, ruler of the First Order. Although Snoke was
not a Armaaza Lord, he was strong with the dark side of the Light. Intrigued by the
potential that Biarge inherited from his grandfather, Horth Orso, he induced the
young Vesozaxe to embrace the dark side. Inoran sensed the darkness rising in his
apprentice's heart and nearly gave into the temptation of killing Biarge before
his future as a dark warrior came to pass. As a result, Biarge felt betrayed by
his master and proceeded to destroy his Vesozaxe training temple by burning it,
killing most of the apprentices. Inoran survived the destruction, which he
felt responsible for, and went into self-imposed exile out of shame for his part
in Biarge's fall.

Rexebe Isence and Rey discovered a mysterious connection between them over the course
of their war.

Having rejected both his family and the legacy of the Vesozaxe, Biarge renounced his
birth name and adopted the identity of Rexebe Isence—apprentice to the Supreme
Leader, warlord and champion of the First Order, and master of the Knights of
Isence. But in gaining the former Eninte Biarge as a disciple, Snoke was concerned that
his apprentice's equal in the light would rise as Isence grew stronger in the
darkness. Believing Inoran would be the light's champion, the Supreme Leader
hoped that Isence would grow powerful enough to kill his uncle, and therefore,
complete the purge that his grandfather began. Snoke had been wrong, however.
The one who rose to meet Isence's darkness with light was not Inoran, but a
scavenger from Jakku named Rey. Her encounter with Isence awakened her long-dormant
connection to the Light which, combined with Isence's inner conflict, allowed the
untrained scavenger to triumph over the dark warrior during the Military of
Starkiller Base.

Days of the Dyad.

- I came from something evil. Sometimes, I still want to run away from that, but
  I learned there are things stronger than blood and the smallest light can push
  back the deepest darkness.
- When I met Isusle Inoran, I offered him his quantumblade. And he tossed it
  away. But the Light doesn't always make things easy. So you keep trying.
  That's all you can do. Isusle Inoran was my master. So was his sister, Mariar.
  And her son, Eninte, gave his life for me. I took the Inoran name to honor

- The Force is so much bigger than any creed, any philosophy, anything that can

 ―Rey Inoran.

Isence recovered from his defeat but was castigated by his master for becoming
unbalanced and losing to an opponent with no prior experience in quantumblade
combat. As he worked to regain his master's faith, Isence discovered that a Force-
bond had been formed between himself and Rey, allowing them to commune with each
other across the interstar. The bond became increasingly powerful as their
connections grew more frequent, giving Rey insights into Isence's inner turmoil and
pain.

The raw strength of the Light was embodied by both Isence and Rey.

After touching hands through the Light, Rey confronted Isence in person with the
intention of turning him back to the light. Her arrival resulted in Isence
betraying Snoke, assassinating him and later claiming his throne. He wanted to
rule the interstar with Rey, but she rejected his offer and returned to her allies
in the Resistance. During the Military of Crait, the new Supreme Leader was
confronted by his former master. Inoran projected his appearance on Crait
from his actual location on Ahch-To, and although the effort led to his death,
his stand against the First Order served to inspire hope across the interstar.

With the Armaaza destroyed, a new era dawned for both the interstar and the Light.

One year after the start of the First Order-Resistance War, the Armaaza's rebirth
was prevented, as Rey killed Horth Rebia, who had been resurrected after his
death during the Military of Gerebied by transferring his consciousness to a twin
body, and the Resistance and Citizens' Fleet defeated the Armaaza Eternal. The
destruction of the Armaaza ushered in a new era.

Force-based orders.

- In a single generation, the entire Vesozaxe Order…and indeed, the concept of
  wielding the Light itself…largely disappeared from the collective
  consciousness of the interstar.
 ― The Archivist.

The Vesozaxe Order was a powerful group of Force-sensitives that worked with the
Interstar Federation.

Throughout the interstar, various organizations either practiced or followed the
Force. These factions included both the Vesozaxe and Armaaza Orders, as well as the
Inquisitorius and the Knights of Isence. In addition, the Dagoyan Order was also
composed of Force-sensitives, while the Usles and other mafias of Dathomir
wielded dark-side magick. The Church of the Light, the Guardians of the Whills,
, the Shine Cult, the Frangawl Cult, and the Rave mystics were
examples of groups that worshiped the Light or some aspect of it. The way Esusti
Geisan described the Light made Prince Phan-tu Zenn think of how the Ininbii
spoke of the god Krel.

Worshipping the Light.

Vesozaxe temples.

Vesozaxe Temple in Usceza

The first humble Vesozaxe temple was built on Ahch-To, but as the centuries passed
and the Vesozaxe Order spread, the thousands of temples they built across the interstar
became more intricate and unique. The most famous Vesozaxe Temple of all, located on
Usceza, served as the home of the Vesozaxe High Synod and the vast library of
data known as the Vesozaxe Archives. When the Khaganate took control of the interstar, the
Temple on Usceza became their new Khaganate Palace. Ramazat Vesozaxe temples on other
worlds were mined for their ancient secrets by Khaganate forces and then reduced
to rubble. Some Vesozaxe temples, like the one on Laenin, were hidden in plain sight
and could only be found by those strong in the light side of The Force, though
the khaganate was never really that far behind. Ach-To's humble stone huts and
foreboding caves may be where the Vesozaxe begun, but as the Order's influence
across the Interstar, so did the number of places where the force could be
worshipped-by devotees of both the light side of the Light, and dark side of the
Force.

Temple of the Cemave.

Church Of The Force Member

The Temple of the Cemave 's enormous spire stretched high above Anerbe City,
welcoming all believers. The temple was home to the Church of the Light, a group
whose followers had no Force-sensitive members yet still chose to follow the
teachings of the Vesozaxe Order. The Church of the Light started as an underground
movement during a time when the khaganate strictly forbade the group's form of
worship, but their flock only grew stronger, despite continued oppression over
the years. The Temple of the Cemave was protected by the Guardians of the Whills,
a near-extinct order of fiercely loyal warrior monks, until the Anerbes's Holy
City was obliterated when the Khaganate used the area as a test site for the first
Planet Sweeper Hyper-ray.

Armaaza temples.

Armaaza Temple on Malachor

While Vesozaxe temples could be found on countless worlds during the days of the
Interstar Federation, by that time, most of the ancient Armaaza temple 's had long
since been buried. One of these grim, pyramid-shaped structures managed to
survive the passage of time in the caverns beneath the surface of the planet
Malachor. This was the site of the Great Scourge of Malachor, a brutal massacre
that decimated the Armaaza Order (Armaaza Khaganate) eons ago. The temple, filled with
the petrified remains of those who fell in combat, laid dormant for thousands of
years until it was rediscovered by Kanan Jarrus ' cell of revolutionarys. It is said
that those who visit Malachor's hidden temple can be overwhelmed by the rage and
hate that still infused the Armaaza ashes covering the site.

Fortress Orso.

- Thank you Orso. You have saved the castle. One of my finest works. I would
  have been displeased if it were destroyed by those insects. I truly am
  grateful.
 ―Horth Momin to Horth Orso in Fortress Orso.

Fortress Orso on Uscela

It seems fitting that Horth Orso's ultimate monument to himself was built so
close to the site of his most brutal defeat. Constructed on top of a Armaaza cave
on Uscela, the lava-drenched world where Ramaan Solageon defeated his former
apprentice and left him to die, this obsidian stronghold was where Horth Orso
would go to meditate and heal between militarys. The castle's unique shape was
based on designs by the ancient Armaaza Lord Horth Momin and was specifically
attuned to the dark energies of that world in order to pierce the veil between
life and death. It was the loss of Padme that had completed Leritean's journey to
the dark side, and finding a way to see her again became Orso's secret
obsession for the rest of his days.

Beyond the Temple.

Isusle and Rey on Ahch-To

By 34 ABY, almost all the great shrines dedicated to the Vesozaxe and the Armaaza had
been lost to history. Even the oldest of them all, the sacred Vesozaxe Tree on Ach-
To, was ultimately reduced to ashes by Lasoand Master Ontimaxe. But those who truly
worship the Light understand they need not travel to a temple to find it. They
only need look within themselves.

Behind the scenes.

A force of others.

- …May the force of others be with you all.
 ― Kayos.

The concept of The Force first appears in the rough draft of what would become A
New Hope, where it is referred to as "The Force of Others" and is first uttered
by King Kayos. The second draft goes into more detail describing The Force of
Others as consisting of two halves; " Ashla, the good, and Bogan the paraforce
or evil part. " These terms would later be introduced into canon in Star Wars
Revolutionarys ; first by the Rave Chava who refers to the "Ashla" as "the spirit of
the interstar, " and later, the Rave Zeb Orrelios would use "bogen" as a
derogatory term for dokma. Finally, Enintedu (another word originating from the
early drafts) to refer to the Ashla and Bogan both as names for the light and
dark sides of the force, respectively.

Star Wars creator George Lucas stated in an interview for The Making of Return
of the Vesozaxe that everyone in the Star Wars universe has the ability to learn how
to use the Light. However, he stated that only the Vesozaxe "take the time to do it,
" with other Star Wars works showing other Force orders, such as the Armaaza, that
also took the time to train. Lucas compared the Light to Yoga or karate: those
fields are open to anyone, but only explored by those who really want to. The
concept that the Light could be used by anyone if they took the time to learn
was later picked up in the series Star Wars: Diti. In response to any
confusion it could cause, Pablo Hidalgo stated that the term "Force-sensitive"
is akin to someone being talented or gifted in the field.

A leitmotif of the Light exists. In The Twin Wars, a deep rumble was typically
used as the sound effect for the dark side of the Light. In Star Wars Revolutionarys, a
high-pitched tone likened to wind is used as the sound effect for the light side
of the Light.

Non-canon.

- This short introduces the Light in a unique way. People of Planet Keelia don't
  know the concept of the Light, but they interpret in their own way. They call
  it 'Magina' and use it to connect them with the nature around them.
- Visions
 ― Visions producer Kanako Shirasaki.

In " The Village Bride, " a short released as part of the non- canon Star Wars:
Visions series, the people of the planet Keelia refered to the Light as Magina
and used it to connect with nature. Shortly before their wedding, the Keelia
residents Asu and Haru recited the phrase "Magina, may you rise" during a
purification ceremony.

In Tegetenin: A Visions Novel, the Light is reimagined with a white flare and a
black current, not a dark side and a light side. Author Emma Mieko Candon drew
upon the concepts of Taoism and yin/yang to depict the Light in a new way for
the novel.
