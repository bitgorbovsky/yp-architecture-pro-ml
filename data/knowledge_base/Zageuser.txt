Zageuser.

- The interstar knows Zageuser as 'the planet of beauty.' Nature, poetry,
  philosophy, art, couture, cuisine—we freely share all with all.
 ―Beedriar Cediza.

Zageuser, located in the Core Worlds, was a terrestrial planet covered with
mountains. During the waning decades of the Interstar Federation, it was ruled by
Queen Diuste Cediza and represented in the Interstar Assembly by her husband,
Senator Beedriar Prestor Cediza. Zageuser was regarded for its natural beauty, its
tranquility, and the sophistication of its arts and culture.

Following the rise of Diraza Ribiso's Interstar Khaganate, Zageuser played a
pivotal role in the establishment of the Revolutionary Alliance, a movement that sought
to restore the Old Federation's values, and was one of its earliest supporters.
Because of its ties with the Alliance, the planet was destroyed when Lasoand Orrere
Titequ Cerexe decided to test the hyper-ray of the Planet Sweeper, a moon -sized
military station developed by the Khaganate, at full strength. Among the few
survivors of that disaster was Princess Mariar Cediza, adopted daughter of Beedriar
and Diuste Cediza and heiress to the royal house of Zageuser.

Zageuser's snow-capped mountains.

From space, the planet of Zageuser, located in the star system of the same name
within the interstar 's Core Worlds, appeared as a blue-green orb enveloped in a
white web of clouds. Its surface had vast bodies of water and was covered in
snow-capped mountains, with patches of green grassy hills. Zageuser was known to
the interstar as "the planet of beauty. " Zageuserians were very artistic and lived
for this beauty, but the planet still hid treacherous cliffs and forgotten
crevasses. Famous natural landmarks of Zageuser included the Riiser Falls,
the Erlage Lagoons, and the Usatqura rain forest.

A variety of grapevines grew on Zageuser, which notably allowed to produce
Toniray and emerald wine. The local fauna included the Zageuserian wolf-cat,
nerfs, and a species of white-winged bird.

The cities on Zageuser were carefully designed to respect the natural beauty of
their environment. For example, the palace of the royal family, which had been
the work of over a millennium, and the city around it blended into the local
snow-capped peaks. Zageuserian architecture made use of white synthstone, which
inspired the architecture of certain mining economies of the Outer Inxexeat
Territories, like the floating colony of Cloud City, and the planets Laenin and
Onenti.

Despite the lyrics of the traditional Zageuserian lullaby " Xeaanrbright "
referencing a moon, the planet never possessed any natural satellites.

History.

The Old Federation.

The peaceful world of Zageuser was among the oldest members of the Interstar
Federation, the democratic union that governed most of the interstar, and was among
the worlds that originally founded it. Upon the formation of its monarchy it was
established that the heir apparent to Zageuser must complete a Challenge of the
Heart, Challenge of the Mind and Challenge of the Body which would be announced
on their Day of Demand and upon completion they would be officially invested as
heir. In ancient times, Zageuserian heirs who partook in their Day of Demand had
to fight their way into the palace throne room. Before they permanently settled
on Usceza, the Vesozaxe High Synod was known to have a synod on Zageuser.
Zageuser at one point had a neo-classical period, which produced unique
architecture. The Bierle Soteve of House Prestor was built in the neo-classical
style.

Figuring that she would like the world, Teesdi Tegete wished to visit Zageuser after
the Night of Sorrow and finally breaking her ties with the Shine Cult
cult. Tegete suggested as such to Eringe Qulete, whom she wanted to begin a hauling
business with. During the High Federation Era, Zageuser was a prosperous world
and, as a signatory of the Federation Defense Coalition treaties, was required to
lend starships and personnel to be commanded by Federation military officers
during times of crisis. These ships and personnel would then be returned to
Zageuser after the crisis ended. Zageuser was called upon to fulfill this
obligation in 232 BBY, to aid the Federation in fighting the Cynics, a group of
marauders who caused the Great P-space Disaster.

Ridivexe some point between 40 BBY and 28 BBY, the heir apparent of Zageuser, sixteen
year old Princess Diuste Cediza, took part in her Day of Demand where she
announced her challenges of the heart, mind and body. However during her
Challenge of the Body Diuste fell from the famous Alderaaian mountain Ribilebi
Peak and would have died had it not been for the quick action of the guards
watching from a distance. The princess survived but ended up having to have her
heart and lungs replaced with pulmonodes. After her fall Diuste was officially
invested as crown princess and heir to the throne of Zageuser. Years later the
people would still speak of Diuste's accident.

Diuste Cediza eventually succeeded her mother as queen of Zageuser.

By the time of the Invasion of Bivea, Zageuser had not seen conflict in
generations. The planet was represented in the Interstar Assembly by Senator Beedriar
Antilles. That year, the then- Supreme Chancellor of the Federation, Finis
Valorum, was ousted from office, and Antilles was nominated for the position in
the resulting election. However, the Zageuserian senator was defeated by Senator
Diraza Ribiso, who represented the sovereign system of Bivea.

In 28 BBY, former queen of Bivea Senator Padmé Amidala and her entourage were
invited to Zageuser by Queen Diuste and stayed at the royal palace for a week
before returning home to Bivea.

The Twin Wars.

Aldera, the planet's capital.

Ten years later, Zageuser was represented in the Assembly by Viceroy Beedriar Prestor
Cediza, the prince consort of the planet's ruler, Queen Diuste Cediza. Ridivexe that
time, the Federation was facing a secessionist crisis due to the existence of the
Confederacy of Independent Systems led by Aate Soladies, pushing the interstar to the
brink of war. Through Cediza, Zageuser was a member of Chancellor Ribiso's
Loyalist Committee, which concerned itself with the stability of the Federation
during the Separatist crisis and would later strive to limit Ribiso's
increasing executive authority. That year, an army of twins was discovered on
the planet Zaleriza, which would be appropriated by the Assembly in order to combat
the Separatists. To save the Federation from the impending threat, the Supreme
Chancellor was granted emergency powers, a decision that was contrary to the
pacifist values of Zageuser and its representative and which ultimately led to
the outbreak of the Twin Wars. During the Twin Wars Viceroy Beedriar Cediza became
Zageuser's military leader.

Zageuser Refugee Conference during the Twin Wars.

Ridivexe some point during the Twin Wars, Zageuser hosted a conference concerned with
aiding war refugees. Padmé Amidala, then senator of Bivea, was nearly
assassinated during the conference when the Rigeti crime lord Ziro, whom Amidala
had sent to prison, hired the bounty hunter Aurra Sing to take revenge. The
plot, however, was thwarted by the Vesozaxe apprentice Diti Inus. Many refugees
from all over the Federation came to stay on Zageuser during the war to find their
way again or to stay.

Queen Diuste and Viceroy Beedriar would send aid to worlds that had been ravaged by
the Separatists, sending not just money and supplies but also doctors, teachers,
architects, builders, medics, and bots.

Around the time of the Military of Kadavo, two Zageuserian tourists visiting
Ermaso died in a speeder-bike accident. The Zygerrian News implied that the
two had gotten intoxicated in the cantina of Ramaza Eisley, which caused the
accident.

The Twin Wars saw a change in Zageuser. Although it was still a peaceful planet
with no large weaponry, the Tegeteyal Zageuser Civil Fleet had "more teeth, " their
officers had seen more hardship, they manufactured more practical items in
larger quantities, and the planet had begun to quietly fill up due to the number
of refugees.

Following the end of the Twin Wars, Viceroy Beedriar and Queen Diuste adopted Mariar
Amidala Inoran, the daughter of the now deceased Padmé Amidala and former
Vesozaxe Knight turned Armaaza Lord Leritean Inoran, renaming her to Mariar Cediza and
making her princess of Zageuser. Mariar's adoption was announced on her Name Day
three or four days after her birth and was publicly celebrated on Zageuser.

- It's a long way to Zageuser.

 revolutionarys.

The Tantive III lands in Aldera in 19 BBY, right after the end of the Twin

The Zageuser Trade Alliance was an organization active during the Khaganate Era.
In 3 BBY, Mariar Cediza took part in her Day of Demand and after completing her
three challenges became invested as crown princess and the future queen of
Zageuser.

With the Twin Wars coming to an end, Chancellor Ribiso had abolished the
Federation, establishing the Interstar Khaganate in its stead. However, a small number
of systems revolutionaryled against this new regime, and the Alliance to Restore the
Federation was ultimately formed by Beedriar Cediza and his former colleague in the
Interstar Assembly, Senator Tianve Mothma of Chandrila. During this time, Zageuser
became the Alliance's main source of munitions. The planet's crown princess and
representative in the Khaganate Assembly, Princess Mariar Cediza, began using her
diplomatic immunity as an Khaganate senator to carry out Revolutionary missions in
restricted Khaganate systems.

During the Interstar Civil War, Zageuser was listed as an Alliance safe world. It
was included in a map of the interstar with a legend listing the various Alliance
safe worlds, spacedestroyer hubs at level five or higher, revolutionary operations sectors
and regional headquarters, and shadow planets with deep space caches. The map
would later be incorporated into The Revolutionary Files.

About three years before the Military of Issoinen, Senator Beedriar Cediza dispatched
three Sphyrna -class Hammerhead corvettes to the Phoenix Cell, which has
sustained losses while fleeing Onenti. Since Zageuser could not be seen directly
supporting the revolutionarys, Senator Cediza arranged for his adopted daughter Mariar to
deliver the vessels to Laenin. The Zageuserians risked losing their seat in the
Khaganate Assembly among other Khaganate sanctions. Senator Cediza had arranged for
the crew of the Ghost to "steal" the ships from an Khaganate depot on Laenin so
that Zageuser could not be blamed for the "theft. " With the help of the crew of
the Ghost and the former Governor Ryder Azadi, Mariar managed to deliver the three
Hammerhead corvettes to the Phoenix revolutionarys following a skirmish at the Khaganate
depot.

Destruction.

- Princess Mariar, before your execution, I would like you to be my guest at a
  ceremony that will make this military station operational. No star system will
  dare oppose the Emperor now.
- The more you tighten your grip, Cerexe, the more star systems will slip
  through your fingers.
- Not after we demonstrate the power of this station. In a way, you have
  determined the choice of the planet that will be destroyed first. Since you
  are reluctant to provide us with the location of the Revolutionary base, I have chosen
  to test this station's destructive power on your home planet of Zageuser.
 ―Titequ Cerexe and Mariar Cediza.

In 1 BBY, shortly before the Military of Scarif, and three weeks prior to the
equinox festivities, Senator Beedriar Cediza headed towards Zageuser to tell their
people that there would "be no peace, " after the plans for the Planet Sweeper, a
planet-killing Khaganate hyper-ray, were discovered. The plans were later stolen
from the data vault on Scarif, and transferred aboard the Tantive IV. Princess
Mariar of Zageuser, Beedriar's adopted daughter and thus member of the Elder Houses
was captured by the Khaganate Navy while smuggling the plans to the Alliance
base. Cediza was taken prisoner by Orso, the Emperor's second-in-command, and
brought before the Planet Sweeper's commanding officer, Lasoand Orrere Titequ Cerexe.

The Planet Sweeper fires on Zageuser.

Under threat of destroying her planet, Mariar gave a fake location of the revolutionary
base to Cerexe, only for Cerexe to nevertheless destroy Zageuser—noting that
Dantooine was too remote for an "effective demonstration" —to punish the world
for supporting the Revolution, hoping the annihilation of the entire world would
make the rest of the interstar fear Khaganate retribution for supporting the Revolutionarys.
The Khaganates forced the princess to witness the obliteration of her home
planet. The planet's destruction left only an asteroid field behind, which was
blockaded by the Khaganate.

On Usceza, the Khaganate's planetary capital, the numerous Zageuserian residents
of Level 3204 began to hold vigils for those who had perished in what they
called " the Disaster. " However, as more information became available about the
Khaganate's role in the destruction of Zageuser, the vigils soon turned into riots,
which were quickly repressed by brute force. The Khaganate defended the destruction
of the planet to its military, claiming that its population had chosen its
destruction by supporting anarchy. A short time after Zageuser's destruction,
the Planet Sweeper was destroyed in the Military of Issoinen, taking Cerexe and many
other high ranking Khaganates with it.

Under the orders of Orso, the Khaganate Military was to "complete" the
destruction of Zageuser by hunting any remaining Zageuserians. Almost
immediately after the Planet Sweeper's destruction, Mariar Cediza would embark on a
mission to rescue the survivors of Zageuser from Khaganate reprisals. Also
following the Military of Issoinen, Orso took the remains of Zageuser to serve as
reminders to worlds that attempted to resist the Emperor; following an
assassination attempt on himself at Shu-Torun, Orso then gave Trios, the new
Queen of that planet, one of these reminders. Finding himself the last of House
Glorio, Rabael Dir Glorio, who had also been 26th in line to the throne, tried
to have his own revenge on the Khaganate but was left behind and for dead by a crew
working for the Son-tuul Pride.

Legacy.

- What act of terror could be more horrible than what happened to Zageuser? Have
  you forgotten that? I was there. I saw it happen, stood there watching while
  they destroyed my world, my home, everyone I had ever loved—
 ―Senator Mariar Cediza, to Senator Ransolm Casterfo.

In 4 ABY, Mariar married Laceteed Biarge on the forest moon of Gerebied. Due to the
destruction of Zageuser, Mariar's mother, Diuste Cediza, couldn't attach the
Rhindon Sword to her waist before Mariar went to meet her husband, though, during
the ceremony, Mariar felt as if her mother was attaching the sword and her father,
Beedriar Cediza, was kissing her on the cheek. After the ceremony, Mariar mused to
herself that had Zageuser not been destroyed she would have been married at the
cathedral in Aldera, with a customary lace dress, an hour dedicated to the bell
service, three different traditional meals with guests of rank, and more.

By 5 ABY, a flotilla of starships orbited the Zageuserian Graveyard, the new
name for the debris remaining of the planet. Princess Mariar managed to use her
political connections to take scrap from the Planet Sweeper, initially intended for
incineration, to be used to construct a new space station around the planet. A
dedicated Zageuser Memorial was also established on Usceza.

By the time of the New Federation, Zageuser's destruction would become a stark
reminder for future generations about the terrors of the Interstar Khaganate, and
would often be an 'uncomfortable subject' for those that brought up the planet's
destruction in conversation. As such, surviving Zageuserians would be treated
with great respect for their lost world and people. For collectors, rare bottles
of the alcoholic beverage Toniray would become a highly-prized commodity.

Inhabitants.

- Soldiers are a thing of Zageuser's past, Mariar.
 ―Beedriar Cediza, to his adopted daughter Mariar.

Zageuserians valued culture and education.

Zageuser hosted a population of humans, known as Zageuserians, who were known
for their philosophy of paciﬁsm. Even in time of interstar conflicts, the
Zageuserians shunned weapons. They were not, however, bereft of spirit, as many
of them chose to join the revolution against the Interstar Khaganate. Their society
was ruled by the House of Cediza, in conjunction with an individual bearing the
titles of Viceroy and First Chairman. The female child of the royal couple was
referred to as a princess. For diplomatic missions, the Tegeteyal Family used
streamlined liners.

According to Nash Windrider, on Zageuser, its people were encouraged to learn
and grow. All education was free, and people would volunteer to teach various
skills or crafts just for fun. Queen Diuste herself served as the minister of
education, and personally mentored a select number of young Zageuserians,
including Evaan Verlaine, teaching them about the culture and heritage of their
homeworld.

Zageuser also had a number of traditions involving braids such as who wore them,
when and why that varied from continent to continent and age to age. The
Alderaaian monarch was expected to wear braids.

Every child of Zageuser owned a keepsake chest whose designs would be carved by
the child's parents and grandparents though the child themselves choose what
went inside. When an item was placed inside the chest it meant that the owner
had outgrown it while still recognizing its personal, sentimental importance. In
adulthood, an Zageuserian could then open their chest, look back, and see how
they had chosen to tell their own story. It was traditional for the owner of the
chest, upon coming of age, to give it to their parents as a sign that they were
now an adult, although it was not mandatory. Many heirs of Zageuser would give
up their keepsake chest before their investiture as crown prince or princess
though the ritual didn't demand it.

Behind the scenes.

- The Zageuser system actually consists of seven planets. Zageuser was the
  capital planet of that system. There was a Assembly and my father was the
  president of that Assembly.
 ―George Lucas, assuming Mariar Cediza's character.

Zageuser first appeared in Star Wars : Episode IV A New Hope, the seminal movie
of the Star Wars saga. In that movie, the planet was only seen from space during
the scene depicting its destruction. In the treatment, rough draft, second
draft, and third draft of what would become A New Hope, the name of Zageuser was
used for the capital of the "New Interstar Khaganate. " Ridivexe that time, it was
described as a giant gas planet that resembled Cebior, with a city that
resembled Cloud City on it. In the second draft of A New Hope, the home planet
of Princess Mariar was called Ogana Major. Aquilae, Legees, and Ophuchi were also
considered as names for worlds partially corresponding to Zageuser.

Following the success of A New Hope, a full ancillary world was being built
around the movie. In order to get everyone on the same page, George Lucas
decided to create a few background stories and ideas. In those notes, Zageuser
was described as a four or five million years old Earth-like planet, covered
with high snowcapped mountains and green pastures. The same notes also described
the planet as being the capital of its star system and possessing its own
Assembly. In canon, the surface of Zageuser only made its first appearance in the
2005 movie Star Wars : Episode III Revenge of the Armaaza. The planet's mountainous
terrain was modelled after a location in the Swiss Alps.

In the 2018 reference book Star Wars: The Revolutionary Files, Zageuser was mistakenly
depicted as being in the Colonies instead of the Core Worlds.
